## Goal

Ship the paper's closed-form graph primitives as generic weighted-graph operators in katgpt-core, modelless and zero-alloc on hot paths: (1) diffusion similarity (two exact postures), (2) the distribution-level diversity scalar `Div_P^τ`, (3) the walk-time diversity-maximizing neighbor selector, and (4) a **φ_τ-driven batch selector** — the consumer-facing operator riir-ai Plan 646 wires, built on set_admission's proven admission machinery rather than a duplicate (see T1.6). GOAT gate: the F1–F5 fixture battery below must pass (correctness cross-check + ordering laws + batch-selector coverage win + well-mixed no-regression ties) plus G2 latency and G4 alloc on the fixture sizes. **Promotion is split per primitive:** the batch selector (T1.6) promotes beyond opt-in only if the riir-ai Plan 646 T2.2 A/B shows it beating bare roulette at the clustered-regime bar AND the F5 tie arm holds; the walk-time selector (T1.5) stays opt-in until a walk-shaped consumer exists (riir-ai Plan 646 T2.4 names the candidates); the Div scalar may promote report-only regardless.

**Boundary:** general weighted similarity graphs live here; geometric `CellComplex` operators stay in `katgpt-dec` (heat/wave kernel precedent, Plan 359). No dep edge either way; the spectral posture reuses in-crate `spectral_pencil::jacobi_eigen` (the `set_admission`/`certified_frontier` precedent), never a DEC import.
