# Research 540: vec2vec — Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings (No-GD Advocate Extraction)

**Status:** DISTILLED — pending owner decision (adversarial-panel No-GD pass: feasibility + location only; coverage/filing owned by the coordinator pass)

**Source paper:** [arXiv:2505.12540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12540) — Jha, Zhang, Shmatikov, Morris (Cornell), *Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings* (vec2vec, NeurIPS 2025). Per-space input adapters A_i (d_i→Z), shared MLP backbone T (Z→Z), per-space output adapters B_i (Z→d_i), trained with GAN losses at BOTH embedding and latent levels + reconstruction + cycle-consistency + pairwise-distance preservation (VSP). Results: cosine 0.85–0.96 to ground-truth target vectors; top-1 match up to 1.0 on an 8192-candidate set; on cross-backbone pairs the OT baselines (Sinkhorn / Gromov-Wasserstein / Hungarian, run WITHOUT oracle pairing) fail to random. Security: translating a stolen vector DB into a known space enables zero-shot attribute inference + zero-shot text inversion (up to 80% of emails leak via Vec2Text).

**Method:** No-GD advocate extraction per research skill §3.5 (Path 0 inventory). Every closed-form / derived / offline-computable / GD-free-iterative component in or around the method, mapped to shipped substrate. ## TL;DR

vec2vec's headline (trained GAN translator between embedding spaces, no paired data) is a riir-train artifact — BUT the paper decomposes into a large GD-free surface, and the workspace is **unusually pre-assembled** for it: paired cross-arch alignment already shipped (katgpt-canon joint-SVD, held-out cos 0.87/0.75 Gemma↔MiniCPM) and its cross-arch direction-transfer demotion (Bench 424–427: four modelless hidden-state methods all failed G6 ≥0.5) is **independent in-workspace replication of the paper's own finding** that linear/OT methods cannot cross architectures without pairing — the exact gap vec2vec's trained non-linear translator fills. The strongest open primitives are NOT the translator: they are (2) the **leakage probe** (attribute-transferability estimator over a foreign/unpaired vector space — defends the DenseEmbedIndex/BonsaiEmbedder assets this stack actually runs) and (1) the **cross-space alignment diagnostics** suite (mostly assembled from already-shipped parts: MAG's TransferMetric suite includes `CkaLinear`/`RbfMmd`/`Wasserstein1d`; `gaussianity_probe`, `effective_rank`, `subspace_phase_gate` all ship). The GD-free translator (whitening→PCA→Sinkhorn→Procrustes) is shippable but is honesty-bounded to the paper's own OT-baseline tier (≈random on cross-backbone) — worth shipping only as an applicability-gated baseline tier that PINS the negative the way Bench 424–427 did. Freeze/thaw packaging of a trained translator is fully precedented (`ArtifactTag::TrainedLora`/`TrainedCheckpoint`, `KarcShard` wout commitment, `StaticCalTable` commitment).

## 2. The four GD-free candidates — feasibility, location, GOAT gate

### Candidate 1 — Closed-form cross-space alignment diagnostics (no pairing)

**Feasible: YES, and mostly pre-assembled.** Components: (a) eigenspectral agreement (compare covariance eigenspectra / participation ratios of the two sample sets — `effective_rank` + `subspace_phase_gate::estimate_intrinsic_dim` ship); (b) distance-distribution agreement (MMD / Wasserstein-1d over pairwise-distance histograms — MAG's `RbfMmd` + `Wasserstein1d` ship); (c) linear CKA over the two Gram matrices when the same n samples pass through both encoders (`TransferMetric::CkaLinear` ships; unpaired variant = spectrum + distance tiers above); (d) kNN-overlap / rank-agreement after ANY candidate map or anchor-relative-representation (the paper's own top-1 metric); (e) sample-sufficiency phase gate so the diagnostics refuse to report on n below intrinsic-dim support (`subspace_phase_gate::phase_transition_gate` ships — this is the applicability-gate pattern).

- **Location:** `katgpt-rs/crates/katgpt-core/src/cross_space/` (new module; consumers: MAG, latent_confounder_audit, riir-neuron-db `substrate_geometry_audits.rs`, riir-engine `latent_functor/`).
- **GOAT gate:** G1 synthetic ground truth — apply a known orthogonal R to a corpus and diagnostics must rank isometry > stretched-anisotropy > permuted-random monotonically (mirror MAG G4 + Bench 194 monotone-in-c discipline); G2 latency (Jacobi eigensolver-bounded; µs at d≤64); G3 no-regression (feature-off count unchanged); G4 0 allocs hot path; count-floor discipline per cfg-gated-target rules.

### Candidate 2 — The leakage probe (attribute-transferability estimator)

**Feasible: YES — the strongest open primitive.** Given unpaired samples F from a foreign vector space + labeled anchors in our space: score how much labeled attribute structure is recoverable. GD-free shape: (i) obtain a candidate map — anchor-relative-representation (8), cluster-level Sinkhorn correspondence (4), or a frozen translator (track c) — (ii) kNN-classify labels of F translated into our space, (iii) leak score = AUC / top-1 vs a label-shuffled permutation control, (iv) report tail-support (n at the reported rank) per the percentile-honesty house rule. **Coverage check per §3.6 signal-diff:** `FaithfulnessProbe` (`katgpt-core/src/faithfulness/probe.rs`, Plan 278) tests whether a consumer causally USES an injected memory segment (intervention delta within one system) — different signal. `latent_confounder_audit` (Issue 194) audits OUR direction vectors for confound-purity using counterfactual encoding slices in one space — different signal, same gate shape. Neither covers cross-space attribute transfer. GAP CONFIRMED.

- **Location:** `katgpt-rs/crates/katgpt-core/src/leakage_probe/` (next to `latent_confounder_audit`); first consumers: riir-neuron-db `DenseEmbedIndex`/`ItemEmbedIndex` freeze-gate advisory (the gaussianity_probe consumer pattern) and riir-rag `BonsaiEmbedder` index audits.
- **GOAT gate (Bench-194-shaped):** G1 planted-leak recovery — foreign corpus = known affine+noise transform of OUR labeled data; probe AUC must match the planted coefficient and report ≈0.5 on an independent foreign corpus; G1b monotone in leak coefficient c; G2 latency (anchor-set kNN, <1µs/call at audit dims); G3 no-regression; G4 0 allocs. Feature `leakage_probe`, opt-in diagnostic.

### Candidate 3 — GD-free unpaired translator (whitening → PCA correspondence → Sinkhorn self-labeling → orthogonal Procrustes)

**Feasible to ship: YES. Expected to REACH the paper's headline: NO.** Every step is closed-form/iterative-deterministic: whitening (eigendecomposition — `katgpt_spectral` substrate), PCA subspace correspondence (`thin_svd_into` ships), Sinkhorn self-labeling (~50 LOC new; no reusable module ships today), `orthogonal_procrustes` (`katgpt-spectral/src/procrustes.rs`, G1-validated ≤1% residual, SIMD'd, 0-alloc). **Honest quality verdict — two independent evidence lines agree it will fail on cross-backbone pairs:** (1) the paper itself: Sinkhorn/GW/Hungarian without oracle pairing fail to random; (2) this workspace: Bench 424–427 demoted the cross-arch canonical claim after FOUR modelless hidden-state methods (Procrustes, length-detrending, joint-SVD subspace, contrastive Recipe D) all failed G6 ≥0.5 cross-arch agreement, and `katgpt-canon/src/lib.rs` L54-62 records "modelless path exhausted" — while the PAIRED joint-SVD path measured cos 0.87/0.75. Orthogonal-map methods cannot cross architectures; the isometry assumption is exactly what fails. Ship it anyway for three reasons: (a) near-isometric pairs DO occur operationally (same model family across versions/checkpoints — index migration without re-embedding), (b) coarse cluster-granularity variant (k-means centroids + Sinkhorn plan + per-cluster translation vectors + RBF/sigmoid routing = the CHaRS machinery, Research 389) extends the envelope beyond global-orthogonal at GD-free cost, (c) **it PINS the negative baseline** the way Bench 424–427 did — any future trained translator must beat it, measured, on the same fixtures (the workspace owns Gemma2-2B d=2304 ↔ MiniCPM5-1B d=1536 locally).

- **Location:** `katgpt-rs/crates/katgpt-core/src/unpaired_transport/` (+ Sinkhorn either here or promoted from it into `katgpt-spectral` for reuse); feature `unpaired_transport`, opt-in.
- **GOAT gate:** G1 synthetic-isometry recovery top-1 = 1.0 (this is the regime where Procrustes is exact); G1b graded-warp envelope — report the degradation curve so the applicability boundary is measured, not asserted; **G1c the honesty gate: real cross-backbone pair (Gemma↔MiniCPM embeddings), top-1 vs random reported — expected ≈random, PINNED**; G2 latency (offline tier, not 50µs hot path); G4 0 allocs steady state.

### Candidate 4 — Freeze/thaw packaging of a trained translator

**Feasible: YES, fully precedented; not a new primitive — a packaging route.** The modelless mandate permits exactly one runtime weight mutation: swapping a frozen snapshot (atomic, versioned, BLAKE3-checked). Precedents: `migration_bundle.rs` `ArtifactTag::TrainedLora` (flat `game_lora.bin`, Issue 381) and `TrainedCheckpoint` (GGUF) — trained artifacts ALREADY cross the freeze boundary by tag; `KarcShard` recomputes its BLAKE3 commitment over `(zone_hash || npc_uuid || config || wout_flat)` on freeze (Bench 152 round-trip bit-identity); `StaticCalTable.commitment`; `MerkleFrozenEnvelope` wraps arbitrary payloads with the tag manifest so thaw knows what's inside. A `TranslatorShard` (A_i, T, B_i weights as flat f32 Pod + dims + commitment) is the same shape as `TrainedLora` + `KarcShard`. Contract check: consumption at inference = pure matvec, zero training — compatible with the katgpt-rs modelless mandate AND with the freeze/thaw runtime contract (readers never see torn snapshots — the FreezeThaw.lean invariant). **GOAT gate:** G1 freeze→thaw→translate bit-identity (cosine to a fixed probe set identical pre/post freeze); G2 forward latency (d→Z→Z→d MLP at d=2304 ≈ 2×5.3M flops ≈ sub-ms — offline RIIR tier, NOT hot path; gate accordingly); G4 alloc-free forward.

## 5. Strongest open primitive (verdict)

**Candidate 2 — the leakage probe** — with candidate 1 as its front-end (same module, shared statistics). Reasons: (i) pure modelless closed-form — no ML infrastructure, ships in katgpt-core beside its gate-shape precedent (Bench 194); (ii) it defends assets the stack actually operates — `DenseEmbedIndex`/`ItemEmbedIndex` (riir-neuron-db) and `BonsaiEmbedder` (riir-rag) are real vector DBs, and this workspace runs multi-encoder inference where cross-encoder vector stores coexist; (iii) vec2vec's 80%-email-leak result makes the threat concrete and citable — this is a security moat story ("our shard/index primitives ship with a measured attribute-leak score"), not a research curiosity; (iv) it composes: MAG metrics for scoring, `subspace_phase_gate` for sample-sufficiency, SipIt for the inversion endgame, candidate 3 or a frozen translator for the map. Candidate 1 alone is cheaper but has a weaker product story; candidates 3 and 4 are supporting cast (pinned negative + packaging route).
