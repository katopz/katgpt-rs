## TL;DR

SpikingBrain's "adaptive threshold spiking" decomposes algebraically into **per-vector mean-abs-scaled integer activation quantization**: `V_th(x) = mean|x|/k`, `s_INT = round(x/V_th)`, `y = V_th·(W·s_INT)` — an exact round-trip (one scalar dequant at the output). The "spiking" framing adds exactly two things beyond quantization: (a) an **event-driven skip story that requires asynchronous neuromorphic hardware** (their own Appendix C concedes memory granularity, control overhead, and dynamic-sparsity caveats; their own §5.2 shows spike coding gives **zero GPU TTFT gain** — integer-activation simulation only), and (b) an **integer count structure** (69% zeros at k=3, 94% of counts ≤7, mean 1.13 spikes/channel, 18.4% channels silent). The published landscape already occupies the class: Xu et al.'s spike-LLM lineage (NSR 2025 "Neuromorphic spike-based LLM"; arXiv:2604.16475 "two-step spike quantization" for activation outliers) and **SpikeQuant** (arXiv:2510.19498, W4A4 dequantization-free, near-FP16 perplexity, ≤4.6× energy) predate or parallel it; public critique lands the same read we did ("looks a whole lot like low-bit quantization of a DNN"). For THIS stack the decisive fact is that the mechanism lands on **already-measured territory**: activation PTQ phases were measured on the league ternary artifact (riir-infer Plan 614/628, Benches 023/033 — a2 catastrophic in both phases, **decode-a4 clean while prefill-a4 loses 5/48 arith, REPORT-ONLY**; NIAH-side clean; a8 ships in prefill; decode activation quantization pinned behind the **never-activation-quantize-decode** posture), and an int8-activation ternary matvec already ships (`ternary_group_q8_act`, katgpt-types Issue 935). **Verdict: modelless GAIN (thin) + training PASS-with-redirect — decision content + consumer-gated option arms, no serving promotion, no new primitive.** The real exports are: the mean-abs-vs-absmax statistic question aimed at a *documented* Q8KV absmax failure, a census shape (spike-count / group-zero spectrum, structural-zero decomposition) that slots into the proven riir-infer census family, four pre-registered refusals that stop a future session re-deriving the dead ends, and a training-recipe set filed as riir-train decision content (its MoE α-rescale law is dormant — Plan 333 dropped its MoE student per its §Non-goals; the recipe arms the future ternary-MoE distillation plan if the neuro-symbolic hypothesis holds).

## 1. Paper core (what it actually establishes)

1. **Adaptive-threshold spiking (§3.3.1).** IF neuron (decay eliminated), soft reset, temporal dimension collapsed to one step for GPU execution: `s_INT = round(v_T/V_th(x))` with `v_T = x` — straight-through estimation if trained; **fully post-hoc otherwise** (128-sample calibration, INT8 weights + spike activations ≈ 2% avg benchmark drop on 7B/76B; §5.5 Table 3). The threshold statistic is the whole novelty: `V_th(x) = mean(abs(x))/k` — outlier-robust (one massive activation barely moves a mean; it *sets* an absmax), so outliers encode as high counts ("burst") instead of crushing everyone else's resolution. `k` is the accuracy–sparsity dial (A.8: k=2 → 73.0% sparsity / −2.6pp avg; k=3 → 69.2% / −1.2pp; k=4 → 66.4% / −0.6pp; k=3 chosen).
2. **Spike coding (§3.3.2).** Exact integer expansion schedules over virtual timesteps: Binary {0,1} (c steps); Ternary {−1,0,1} symmetric signed-digit (~halves steps + firing rate; their measured 18.4%-silent / 1.13-mean-spike stats are bitwise-ternary class); Bitwise `Σ 2^(t−1)·s_t` (count 256 = 8 steps vs 256/128; variants pure / bidirectional ±1 / two's complement). All closed-form, mechanically testable.
3. **Energy claims (§5.5 + App. C).** 43.5× vs FP16 MAC / 6.8× vs INT8 MAC — **on idealized asynchronous neuromorphic hardware**; Appendix C names the unmet preconditions (channel-granularity memory fetch, async control overhead, dataset-dependent sparsity). On synchronous GPU: zero speedup, by their own measurement.
4. **Conversion training (§3.1–3.4).** Attention-map correspondence init (linear attention = low-rank view of `QK^T`; SWA = sparsified + recency bias; reuse pretrained QKV projections), non-negative QK activation (ReLU @ 7B / sigmoid @ 76B), new params low-rank, long-context extension folded into CPT stages (8k→32k→128k, ~150B total ≈ 2% of from-scratch), full-parameter training (KD available but declined). B.1 ablation (5B tokens, Qwen3-1.7B): pure SWA(4k) ≈ FA on LM (MMLU 61.74 vs 61.78) but recall collapses outside the window (S-NIAH-8k 47.6 vs 98.4); pure LA worst LM (MMLU 39.87); hybrid LA:SWA 1:1 best trade-off (MMLU 56.08, S-NIAH-4k 96.83).
5. **MoE upcycling (§3.2).** Replicate dense FFN → N experts + S shared, rescale expert outputs by `α = (1/(S+k/N))^(1/3)` (their S=1,k=1,N=16 → α≈0.98) so the upcycled MoE ≈ dense FFN at init; stochastic routing breaks symmetry. **Negative result: partial random expert init did NOT help** — copy + rescale won.
6. **Negative results (§6).** Concatenated-short-context long-context training gave limited gains (native long data needed); 8k reasoning SFT truncated CoT (32k needed); seq-parallel async instability fixed with explicit syncs.

## 2. Distillation

### 2.1 The algebraic decomposition (the load-bearing reframe)

`s_INT = round(k·x/mean|x|)` followed by `y = (mean|x|/k)·W·s_INT` is **per-vector mean-abs integer activation quantization** — a *statistic choice* (mean-abs) plus a *grid choice* (integer counts, ~3-bit class at k=3) plus *saturation semantics* (outliers → high counts, not clipping). The spiking narrative is load-bearing ONLY where MACs are actually skipped (async hardware) — everywhere else it is a quantization format wearing neurons. This is not our invention: public critique (r/newAIParadigms on the tech report) and the SpikeQuant paper both treat it as low-bit activation quantization; the paper's own §5.2 concedes the GPU arm is integer-simulation-only. Distilling it AS a quantization statistic is what makes it comparable against our measured territory.

### 2.3 Signal-diff per closest cousin (the §3.6 discipline)

- **`ternary_group_q8_act` (katgpt-types Issue 935, verified at source) vs spike coding:** same operand shape (integer activations + scales feeding a ternary matvec); different statistic (**absmax-per-128-group with ±127 saturation** vs **mean-abs-per-vector with unbounded integer counts**) and different zero semantics (dense SDOT exploits none of the 69% zeros). The statistic delta is real but its accuracy consumer is closed (decode-a4 clean and the a8 control at |Δ| = 0 on the league artifact — Benches 023/033; prefill a8 ships) and its speed consumer is closed (dense SDOT beats gather at 31% density; weights dominate bandwidth). What survives: the **group-zero-rate question** (item 3's prerequisite) — absorbed into the census arm.
- **The DQ measured territory (riir-infer Plan 614/628, Benches 023/033 — verified in-repo):** activation PTQ phases were measured on the ternary PQ2_0 league artifact; a2 catastrophic in BOTH phases, **decode-a4 clean (0.9583 = base) while prefill-a4 loses 5/48 arith (0.854 — REPORT-ONLY: every axis read INADMISSIBLE per the frozen D6 table, Bench 023)**, NIAH-side activation-a4 clean (Bench 033), the a8 control passes (|Δ| = 0 items), the KV-store axis NULL, and the **never-activation-quantize-decode** posture pinned. Spike coding (~a3-with-sparsity at k=3) sits at or BELOW the a4 rung that already costs arith accuracy at prefill (report-only): no accuracy headroom anywhere, and no bit-budget pressure (weights, not activations, own the bytes). Any spike-format arm must enter THROUGH this instrument's amendment path with per-phase R published first (Plan 614's standing rule) — pre-registered in the issue.
- **`lif_graph` (katgpt-rs Issue 763 / Bench 760 / Research 379):** the shipped event-driven cousin — fixed-threshold LIF reservoir over an external graph, exact-parity active set (bitwise fixed-point quiescence), G4-alloc event loop. 1. **Q8KV anatomy join:** 605's weight-derived MA census × the Q8KV absmax collapse × the mean-abs statistic → a *predictive* statistic-choice instrument: the census that already predicts MA channels could co-predict the per-block scale damage and adjudicate mean-abs vs absmax vs sink-sidecar per family — before any kernel changes. 2. **Group-zero census → the skip ceiling:** our packed ternary layout is 128-group aligned (the q8-act GROUP_SIZE); a census measuring **whole-group-zero rates** (structural post-GLU dead mass ρ₀ + threshold-induced zeros at candidate k) turns the paper's unexploitable 69% element sparsity into a *group-granularity* skip ceiling that IS coherent with our matvec shapes (`matvec_pair_into`, Bench 879's fused lane). This is the only form of the paper's headline that can survive our hardware — and it is measurable for the price of one census pass.
3. **Exact-integer matvec composition:** ternary weights (PQ2_0) × integer spike activations = multiply-free ±add accumulation with **order-independent bit-exactness** — a strictly stronger determinism property than Issue-940's pinned-order floats. The mechanism shape is already corpus-owned (tunazip rule, Batch 418); the *composition* on the serving lane stays decision content until the census creates a consumer.
