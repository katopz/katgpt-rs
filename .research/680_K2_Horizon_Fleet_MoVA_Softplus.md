## TL;DR

A six-model fully-open fleet (0.9B dense → 375B-A23B MoE) with every checkpoint, log, dataset and pipeline released. Three things matter to us: **(1) MoVA** — MoE applied to the attention VALUE projection (top-4-of-64 linear+SiLU value experts, mixed output cached → KV-cache byte-identical to GQA), the 36B-A4B flagship variant landing within 1.2–3.7 points of its own 32B dense sibling at ~1/7 active params; **(2) softplus attention output gate** — replacing the sigmoid output gate with a β-anchored softplus (β = ln 2 ⇒ softplus(0) = 1 ⇒ identity at init; unbounded above ⇒ can amplify, which sigmoid structurally cannot); **(3) Uno at fleet scale** — the 2609.04010 system shipped as released adapters on the 0.9B and 7B with the full training recipe published. ## The two new architecture primitives (distilled)

### MoVA — Mixture-of-Value Attention (§2.2, 36B-A4B only)

`V(x_t) = Σ_{i∈top-k} p_i(x_t) · silu(x_t W_v^(i))` — the single value projection becomes a routed mixture over V = 64 value experts, top-4 active per token. Fleet config (Table 1): value experts 64/4 active, each expert produces values for ALL 8 KV heads jointly (not per-head experts).

- **KV-cache invariance is the load-bearing property:** only the mixed `v_t` is cached, byte-same size as standard GQA. Routing happens BEFORE the cache, so FlashAttention/GQA/sparse attention compose unchanged.
- **The SiLU is not decorative:** without it the mixture collapses algebraically to `x_t (Σ_i p_i W_v^(i))` — an input-dependent LINEAR map whose only nonlinearity is the routing weights. Each per-expert SiLU restores per-expert nonlinearity.
- **Measured:** 36B-A4B pre-training loss closely tracks the 32B dense sibling (same 22T corpus, same recipe); at 1.1M steps trails by 1.2–3.7 points on MMLU/GSM8K/HellaSwag/HumanEval with ~1/7 the active parameters (4.7B vs 32.2B).
- **Quantization posture (§10):** MoVA released FP8-only — value experts, attention, shared expert, routers stay BF16; only routed FFN experts quantize. The MLX 4-bit recipe quantizes routed+shared experts 4-bit / attention+dense+embed+lm_head 8-bit (group 64) / routers bf16 (+1.2% WikiText-2 PPL, 59.6 tok/s @ 26.5 GB on M4 Max).

### Softplus attention output gate (§2.3, 36B-A4B only)

Gated attention `Y = (f(QKᵀ/τ)V) ⊙ γ`, `γ = φ(XW_γ + b_γ)`. Prior convention: sigmoid (or SiLU; Qiu et al. 2025: sigmoid slightly wins). K2's argument, accepted: **sigmoid is bounded (0,1)** — at init it sits at ≈0.5 and HALVES the attention output; even at max it can only recover the ungated output, never amplify. The Ma et al. 2023 expressiveness result (single-head gated attention ≈ multi-head) requires the gate to represent the multi-head/single-head RATIO, which exceeds 1. Softplus `softplus(x;β) = (1/β)ln(1+e^{βx})` with **β = ln 2** gives `softplus(0) = 1` → the gate is ≈identity at init (gated ≈ standard attention at zero pre-activation), strictly positive (pure element-wise scaling), unbounded above (can amplify).

### Grouped RMSNorm (§2.1, minor)

RMS computed per group of d/k dims instead of the whole vector — bounds each normalized output element to (−√(d_g), √(d_g)) instead of (−√d, √d), capping the magnitude growth that wide models otherwise allow (d = 8192 ⇒ RMSNorm can emit ≈90-magnitude elements). Applied to 3.7B/7B/32B/36B-A4B (1–4 groups). Training-stability primitive only.

## Release-landscape rows (lane-intel, not techniques)

- **Routed-experts-only quantization (§10):** the 375B holds 97% of params in routed experts; quantizing only them captures ~all of the storage reduction (706→364 GiB FP8), while extending FP8 to attention+dense layers saves 6 GiB more and LOSES accuracy — routed-only matches BF16 on the 3-benchmark mean (+0.05). **Independent production-scale confirmation of our lossy-surface promotion rule** (gate on deployed-path behavior per family; demote the family that drops).
- **NVFP4 expert calibration trick:** per-tensor activation scales calibrated by routing EVERY token through EVERY expert (rarely-routed experts would otherwise miss their largest inputs and clip at inference); the cost (coarser small-value rounding) accepted deliberately. Relevant to any future MoE quantization lane in riir-infer. Also: 4/8 and 4/16 activation variants erase the 4/4 loss (−0.07 / +0.75 vs −0.74) — they ship 4/4 anyway for Blackwell FP4 compute.
- **KV dominance at long context:** at 512K, a 7B sequence holds 72 GiB of KV vs 17 GiB of weights; every model's decode rate falls sharply with context (their Fig. 47). They promise runtime KV compression as separate future work. Corroborates the value of our Q8KV/kv-eviction/sink-audit lanes — long-context KV, not weights, is the serving wall.
- **ISO-RAM expert merging (§6.4):** five RL domain experts merged at 375B scale — ISO-merger (singular-vector tangent-space merge at the base, overlap-aware coefficients via a small K×K system, base spectrum kept; measured: rebuilding specialists from base singular values misses median 1.4% of each update; matched singular-vector angles 1.6°–3.8° median) for attention + shared experts; RAM (element-wise update detection |τ|>ε, average only over specialists that touched an entry, single-specialist updates at full strength) for routed experts/router/norms. Merged model retains most expert gains and BEATS individual experts on several benches (BrowseComp 68.1 vs best expert 62.5; Terminal-Bench 46.1 with no dedicated expert). Extending ISO-merger to routed experts gave no measurable gain.
- **RL360 GRPO hygiene (§6.1–6.2):** failure-aware batch construction — infrastructure failure ≠ policy outcome: classify rollout outcomes by exit status; a group enters the batch only if NO member failed AND rewards are not all identical; the WHOLE group drops (not just failed members — failures correlate with trajectory shape, so survivors would bias the baseline); truncation keeps its reward in the group statistics but masks the loss. Plus: fully-async training with partial rollouts at weight pushes (trajectory resumes under new policy mid-episode, per-token IS correction, staleness cap 5, IS ratio cap 2.0), sequence-mean loss, dynamic sampling per DAPO, entropy bonus vs mode collapse. **Our GRPO family (nf_grpo.rs / degrpo.rs / pooled_grpo.rs) has none of the infra-failure separation** (grep-verified this session: only std+eps normalization and offline pooling).
- **FTPO anti-doom-loop (§6.5):** small models loop on long generations post-SFT; a brief Final Token Preference Optimization stage on preference pairs mined from the model's OWN looping outputs removes runaway generations while keeping benchmarks flat (largest gains on agentic tasks where one runaway stalls an episode).
- **Scaling rules (§3.1):** η ∝ N^(−1/2) (η·√N ∈ 6.8–8.6e-4 across the fleet); AdamW timescale τ_epoch ∝ TPP^(−1/2); the production batch halved on scaled-down batch-size tests; LR kept at peak through pre-training with the decay realized by AVERAGING final checkpoints (Tian et al. WSM) before mid-training; loss-spike monitor = robust local z-score (rolling median + MAD, flag z>5, episode until z<2 ×3 steps) with restart-from-latest + manual deeper rollbacks, and repeated same-checkpoint resumes left UNGUARDED deliberately (a replay spike at the same step flags the checkpoint for an engineer).
- **Synthetic-data doctrine (§4):** ~10T synthetic pre-training tokens; **no verification** — "every token is a label for its context; an error is one mislabeled token" (strict verification filtering would discard thousands of valid tokens per trajectory to remove a few mislabeled ones; only multi-token corruption classes are filtered); diversity via combinatorial knobs + document grounding, MEASURED by a custom lossless compressor (WZIP compression ratio as the diversity proxy — the final pipeline tracks natural-data compression trends, the knob-less v0 did not); the production mix chosen by benchmark scores PREDICTED at production scale from small-model scaling laws + loss→score sparse regression — the prediction REVERSED the measured ordering (web-heavy predicted best at 31.2B while SFT-heavy measured best at 1.5B; they trained web-heavy and it won).
- **Board observation:** the JDI (riir-rethink `.research/data/jdi_index_release-v4_2026-10-22_*.json`) already carries `IFM/K2-Horizon-3.7B` as a lab-scored base run (balanced_raw 57.49 / balanced_skill 35.41 / public_skill 42.68). The fleet's other five sizes are candidate entrants. Board tracking is decision-index-beat's lane — recorded here as a pointer only, no rethink file.

## Prior-art landscape (§4 searches run this session; no novelty claimed — the paper owns its techniques)

- **MoVA class prior:** SwitchHead — "Accelerating Transformers with Mixture-of-Experts Attention" (Csordás, Piękos, Irie, Schmidhuber; IDSIA; arXiv:2312.07987, v1 2023-12-13, NeurIPS 2024 — abs page fetched this session to verify the attribution; a first draft of this row mis-attributed it to a Google team and the verdict reviewer caught it) — quoted finding from that paper: *"Having key and query experts seems to be unnecessary. In fact, without the output and value experts, they even underperform the dense baseline"* — value/output experts are where MoE-attention pays. VideoEditor-MoE (2026-11) adds routed key–value experts. K2's specific cell (top-4-of-64 LINEAR+SiLU value experts jointly over all KV heads + KV-cache invariance + a same-corpus dense-pair at 36B scale) is their validated combination, not a new class.
- **Softplus output gate prior:** 1a3orn "Softplus Gated Attention > Sigmoid" (2025-09-03) + "More Evidence For Softplus Gated Attention" (2025-09-24) — measured claims (~10–30% faster to equal loss vs sigmoid-gated, ~25–50% vs non-gated). **The K2 paper does not cite it** (its gate section cites Ma et al. 2023 and Qiu et al. 2025 only). K2's contribution narrows to the β = ln 2 identity-at-init anchor + the Ma-ratio expressiveness argument + fleet-scale validation.
