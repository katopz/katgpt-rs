## TL;DR

Kolibri 1.1 replaces its ten dense attention layers with YASA — a two-step hierarchical sparse attention scored by a **tied indexer** (the main attention's key-value head 0 + its 12 query heads; zero separate indexer weights, no KL loss), with **reindexer layers** reusing an earlier indexer's pool, an **NVFP4 KV cache** (QAT), **cross-layer KV sharing** over a closed-form init (Procrustes key alignment + Eckart–Young value projection + output absorption), and **prefill exit** (only the first 30 layers run the full sequence). Up to 14× decode throughput at 1M context. Everything is class-covered published work (NSA/DSA/MoBA/InfLLM-V2/CLA/YOCO/xKV/NVFP4-KV); the value for our stack is two modelless extractions, both filed as PoC arms in katgpt-rs Issue 1250: (a) the zero-param tied indexer as a **cheaper-indexer challenger** to the landed riir-infer sparse-prefill league arm, pre-registered against Bench 612's measured coarse-to-fine recall deficit, and (b) the **closed-form cross-layer KV-sharing init kit**, whose two math halves already ship in `katgpt-spectral` — consumer-gated on a continued-pre-training lane we do not run.

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, inference-time):** the tied scorer `s_j = mean_h ReLU(q_h·k_j)` over ONE existing head group is a zero-weight indexer: no KL distillation, no two-stage schedule, no aux objective — and it costs 1/8 of main-attention FLOPs/cache-read (×4 from one of four head-groups, ×2 from omitting the value sum). The KV-sharing init is pure closed-form algebra our substrate covers: `orthogonal_procrustes` (keys; logits invariant under a shared Q/K rotation) + rank-n SVD projection of the stacked `O_m·V_m` products (values; Eckart–Young) + least-squares `C` absorption into output matrices + a rank-one reference correction. YASA measured the recipe's pre-training value honestly: naive-copy init +2.31 bits/byte → aligned 1.15 → +reference correction 0.62.

## 1. Paper core findings (what YASA actually is)

Kolibri 1: 50 layers, 40 sliding-window + 10 dense. Kolibri 1.1 converts the 10 dense layers to YASA sparse:

- **Tied indexer** — no separate indexer weights: key-value head 0 (of 4) and its 12 query heads (of 48) score candidates. Score per key = `mean over the 12 heads of ReLU(q_h·k_j)`; coarse step scores each 16-token block by its **compressed key** (mean of the block's 16 keys, cached). Training signal arrives through the softmax backward pass itself — no KL-to-attention-distillation loss, no two-stage schedule (the blog contrasts this with DSA/Qwen3.8-Next/MiniMax indexer training). Prior art the blog itself names for tied/main-attention scoring: MoBA (block scored by query-key product with mean block keys, no params) and InfLLM-V2 (scores averaged keys with main attention weights). YASA's deltas: ONE group's selection shared by ALL four groups; ReLU-mean (no cross-key normalization needed); fine step selects single tokens.
- **Hierarchical selection** — coarse: 1,024 blocks × 16 tokens → pool 16,384; fine: 2,048 tokens from the pool. Only the coarse step scales with context (on a 16× shorter sequence). Key compression cut an indexer layer's coarse selection 784 µs → 128 µs per decode step at 1M (B200, batch 64). Indexer-vs-reindexer selection: 1.29 ms → 0.72 ms per decode step at 1M.
- **Reindexers** — indexers at layers 5, 15, 20, 30; the other six sparse layers reuse the previous indexer's pool and re-select with their own queries. Prior art cited: IndexCache (adjacent-layer selections overlap 70–100% in DSA models), LatentIndex (pool-reuse keeps most recall of full-context selection), TidalDecode (one unchanged selection reused over many layers reduces quality).
- **Softmax attention** over gathered 2,048 selected + 8 sink tokens + 128 most-recent = 2,184 tokens (fixed length).
- **NVFP4 KV cache** (from FP8; NVFP4 chosen over MXFP4 for finer block scales; global scale omitted), QAT with fake-quant in the forward (gradient passes through unchanged; the indexer runs NVFP4 too so selection sees inference rounding). Sliding-window layers keep BF16 (bounded, negligible at long context; no FP4 speedup observed in their window kernels). Cache dequantized in-kernel; attention runs BF16.
- **Cross-layer KV sharing** — producers only at layers 5, 20, 25, 30 (4 shared full-length caches, down from 10); six consumers read a producer's cache but keep their own Q/O matrices (so per-layer token sets still differ); layer 50 reads layer 5's cache (slight quality win — last layer gets lower-level context representations). Sharing does not change per-step cache-read; it raises request concurrency.
- **Init for sharing (the appendix — the modelless math)** — keys: logits invariant under a shared rotation `Q^T K = (RQ)^T (RK)`; alternate optimal per-member rotations `R_m = argmin_{R^T R=I} ||R K_m − K̄||_F` with `K̄ ← mean R_m K_m`; rotate each member's queries by the same `R_m`. Values: stack all members' `O_m,h·V_m` into `A`; the rank-n projection (Eckart–Young) gives the shared row space; each member's `C_m = V_m V̄^T (V̄ V̄^T)^{-1}` is absorbed into its output matrices. Reference correction: split off the average value, rank-one output-matrix update for the consumer's `O_c(v̄_p − v̄_c)` shift. Measured: naive copy **+2.31** bits/byte → key+value alignment **1.15** → +reference correction **0.62**.
- **Prefill exit** — all full-length caches produced in the first 30 layers, so prefill runs only those (1.87B of 3.44B active params) over the full sequence; the remaining 20 layers see only the last 8,193 tokens (the sliding-window receptive field). YOCO-class idea applied to continued training.
- **Costs at 1M** — attention FLOPs/token 129G → 1.9G; cache read per decode step 10.8 GB → 0.09 GB; KV cache per sequence 10.8 GB → 2.8 GB.
- **Serving** (4× B300, vLLM 0.33, EP4, prefix caching): prefill 1.7× @256k, 4.4× @1M; **decode 1.6× @16k, 14× @1M**. At ≤16k the advantage is modest — the selection overhead is only repaid at long context.
- **Quality** — base models −1.5 (EN) / −1.7 (DE) overall; post-trained 73.2% vs 66.6% (their model-card suite; includes post-training improvements, not an isolated YASA ablation). RULER tracks Kolibri 1 to 1M with small losses at 32k/64k/256k+.
- **Continued pre-training** — 805B tokens (~¼ of Kolibri 1's 3.4T mid-training), phases 64k → 192k, long-context stage 256k, LR warmup 50 steps then constant, **linear merge of 40 checkpoints** (all 32 mid-training + all 8 long-context). The architecture change degrades quality without CPT — this is the load-bearing training dependency.

**Signal-diff record (§3.6, one read per cousin):**
- `flashmemory_sparse::DualEncoderIndexer` — consumes a **separate trained indexer's** Q/K projections (opt-in feature, weights loaded from training); YASA's tied scorer consumes the **main attention's own** group-0 Q/K — the delta is zero indexer weights + the training-signal route, not the scoring primitive itself.
- `pyramid_topk` / Bench 612 — consumes mean-pooled coarse keys and prunes per LSE level; **measured −0.133 Recall@8 at 32K+ vs single-level exact-LSE**. YASA's coarse step produces a *pool* (16,384) that per-layer reindexers re-select from — a different selection contract — but on an untrained model the Bench-612 deficit is the honest prior for any coarse-first arm.
- `kv_share.rs` — merges K and V **within one layer** (cache-halving at load); YASA shares K/V **across layers** with alignment. Distinct axis, compose-not-merge.
- Issue 925 Procrustes probe — orthogonal Procrustes bases fitted over attention tensors as a *routing summary* operator; YASA applies Procrustes over *key matrices across layers* as a *weight-restructuring* init. Same math, different surface.
- `dash_attn` — chunk-level routing + α-entmax activation sparsity; different mechanism family (activation-side, not selection-side).

But the logit-invariance argument requires nothing between projection and dot product — on qwen3.8's full-attention layers (QK-RMSNorm gain, then RoPE) a free orthogonal R does NOT commute, and the measured loss delta would mostly be that corruption. The lawful forms: per-frequency 2×2 block-diagonal rotations (the RoPE-commuting subgroup, gain uniform within each pair or folded first), or the non-RoPE part of a partial-rotary head. `procrustes.rs` ships no constrained variant — if Issue 1250 T6 confirms the gap, the constrained Procrustes is a katgpt-spectral issue, never a local fork. Measurement posture: transform the f16/f32 DEQUANTIZED weights (the league checkpoint is Q4_K_M — re-quantization after the transform is a noise confound; the quantized arm is reported separately), and the bar is RELATIVE (aligned ≤ 0.5× naive-copy loss delta, CI over eval slices) — YASA's 2.31 → 0.62 bits/byte is their architecture's reference point, never our gate.
