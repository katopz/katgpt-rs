## TL;DR

Epiplexity (S_T, the structural information a compute-bounded learner extracts) becomes an **online training signal** two ways. **EpiSelect**: fit a cross-domain scaling law per domain, `L̂_m = ε_m + β_m·(Σ_k γ_m,k·n_k)^(−α_m)` (γ simplex-normalized influence; refit every 1000 steps, log-Huber, Dirichlet-init 729-pt grid, ~12 s/fit, <5% overhead), take the closed-form marginal epiplexity gain per domain, set sampling weights `π_k ∝ exp(gain/τ)` with momentum + clipping. Beats ADO (SOTA mixer) zero-shot at 124M and 1.3B on Common Pile. **EpiGen**: reward a generator with the learner's loss drop **on a reference buffer that is never trained on** (it is only the prequential reference distribution), REINFORCE with EMA baseline. Two negative controls pin the design: a PPL reward collapses to repetition (4-gram rep 0.47); a buffer-less reward collapses to adversarial noise (frozen-scorer ppl 169.7); the buffer-grounded reward stays natural (ppl 58, rep 0.007) and improves GLUE +2.8 with zero real tokens. Findings: epiplexity↔OOD r=0.88 (weight norm r=0.01); The Pile is saturated as a selection benchmark (largest domain alone beats SOTA selectors); 25–50% synthetic/real mix beats either alone; gains require pretrained (warm-started) initialization.

**The paper's deepest modelless import is the decomposition: marginal value = headroom (L̂−ε) × exposure (n_m/effective count) × influence (γ_m,k)** — every factor closed-form; the saturation gate falls out of the same formula (headroom→0 ⇒ no allocation helps).

## Signal-diffs (§3.6) on the coverage dismissals

- **vs shipped `EpiplexityEstimator`/`LossCurveTracker`:** consumes the AREA estimate of a single stream for relevance blending vs cross-domain fits consumed for ALLOCATION across domains. The shipped module has no derivative, no γ, no weights. (Read at `crates/katgpt-pruners/src/epiplexity/loss_curve.rs:76` — running-min area only.)
- **vs default-on `epiplexity_bandit` (`EpiplexityArmHeuristic`/`select_with_epiplexity`):** consumes BINNED S_T/H_T as bandit CONTEXT — a warm-start hint and a fixed 0.1 arm bonus over 4 planning arms; the fit/derivative/weights machinery is absent. This note's signal is continuous, curve-fitted, and allocation-facing; the bandit's is discrete, post-hoc, and choice-facing. Complementary, not duplicate.
- **vs learning-progress curricula (Graves 2017 / TSCL 2017 / ALP-GMM 2019, closest published class):** raw windowed progress/slope as the allocation reward vs a FITTED per-domain scaling law whose closed-form marginal carries a floor ε (saturation semantics the raw-slope methods lack), an influence matrix γ (cross-domain credit they lack), and an MDL-derived target (epiplexity) rather than raw gain.
- **vs refine Elo/active-set demote:** per-rule outcome windows (post-hoc fix quality, per-rule granularity) vs corpus-level structural-gain prediction (pre-hoc, curve-fit-derived, per-DOMAIN granularity). Complementary: outcomes judge landed rules; headroom orders the NEXT batch.
- **vs coverage_gap_event:** coverage counts of lint surface vs learnable-structure gain — a fully-covered domain can still be saturated (headroom≈0), and a low-coverage domain can be saturated too. Different axis.
- **vs ADO (published):** ADO's preference has a heuristic credit term λ_k and no cross-domain influence modeling (paper App. B.2); EpiSelect models γ and beats it (Table 1). The class (training-time scaling-law mixers) is taken; our extension is the surface (selection-time, modelless) and the epiplexity target.
- **vs reflex corpus-cap levers (Bench 003, NEGATIVE):** those capped retrieval fan-out by corpus size; this allocates GROWTH/admission budget by predicted gain. Different decision point; the negative is imported as family caution, not coverage.
