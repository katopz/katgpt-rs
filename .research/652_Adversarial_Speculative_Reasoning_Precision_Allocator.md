## TL;DR

The paper measures that speculative-decoding verification is **lossless in distribution but not uniform in precision**: logit noise grows ~4% per slot inside a verification block (slot 0 == single-token decode; >2-nat tails ~2× by block end), and context *written* through verification reads back with 10–20% excess extreme (>4-nat) deviations after ~30 agent turns. It exploits this with a closed-form handle — `Pr[reject] = TV(p,q)`: the draft controls *where blocks end* — and trains the draft (reverse-DPO, 0.06–0.28% of hosted params) to be **rejected** at decision points, so critical tokens are resampled by the target and committed at the most precise slot. DeepSWE +7.0/+4.0 pts at ≤2% speed cost; drafts trained toward the *preferred* side HURT, proving placement (not draft quality) is the mechanism.

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, inference-time):** every ingredient of the placement mechanism is runtime-computable without training on our stack, because the keyed Gumbel walk makes forced rejection *exactly* the organic resample (distribution-exact and per-seed byte-exact by the coupling theorem). What survives as OUR novel delta after the published-prior-art narrowing: the **per-slot cross-path delta census fused with the per-format precision cliff** (`precision_cliff`/`margin_equilibrium`, Issue 959 at `../crates/katgpt-core/src/softmax_fix.rs`) to *derive* an acceptance-gate threshold, plus deferral semantics (move the block boundary so the critical token is *written* at slot 0). The paper is simultaneously a **threat model for our own lane**: its §3 measurements target exactly the quantity our DFlash2 lane's byte-exact-vs-serial hard gate depends on — a quantity verified so far only to a 2048-token horizon (Bench 973), and plausibly absent altogether if our kernels are batch-invariant. A minutes-cheap bitwise check (Issue 982 T0) decides which world we are in; the census + soak covers the rest.

## 1. Paper Core (compact)

**§3 — Context precision, measured.**
- Setup: GLM-5.3 (NVFP4 weights, FP8 KV, DSA, DFlash2 8-slot blocks) and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (DSPARK, 6-slot blocks) on SGLang/B200. Metric: |Δlog p| in nats across R=4 repeated forwards on identical prefix+KV; tails at 1/2/3/4 nats. Nucleus-flip stakes: a 1–2 nat shift changes nucleus membership in 12% of samples, 2–3 nats in 47%, >3 nats in 84%.
- Finding 1 (per-slot growth): slot-0 noise == decode noise (0.473 vs 0.478 nats on GLM-5.3); slope +0.0195 nats/slot (95% CI [+0.0174, +0.0214]) ≈ 4% of slot-0 level per slot; last slot 34% noisier; >2-nat tokens 3.2% → 6.2%, >3-nat 1.1% → 2.1%. DeepSeek: half the absolute noise, the same relative ~4%/slot. Mechanism: a slot-s token attends to KV entries of slots 0..s−1 computed in the SAME launch — each repetition re-rounds them, so slot s inherits accumulated variation. Slot 0 reads only cached entries, hence == decode.
- Finding 2 (long horizon): context written through block verification vs single-token decoding (identical token sequence, different write path) is indistinguishable for ~30 turns, then shows 10–20% excess of >4-nat deviations (ratios 1.09 turns 31–50, 1.16 turns 51–70 [0.98,1.36]; independent build 1.20 [1.03,1.40]). 96 tasks, 6.38M scored assistant tokens per build.

**§4 — Adversarial speculation.**
- `Pr[reject] = 1 − Σ min(p,q) = TV(p,q)` — closed form. Rejection → target resamples from the residual `r ∝ max(0, p−q)`; the resampled token becomes slot 0 of the next block. The draft cannot change WHAT the target emits (exactness preserved); it changes WHERE blocks end.
- Decision points = "committing sentences" (judge = the target model itself, ≤35 words verbatim). Decision-frame taxonomy: **principle/evidence frames correct ~70% vs likely/risk/simpler ~36%** (GLM-5.3). Second preference (DeepSeek): run-a-test vs move-on — failures concentrate in decisions taken without validation.
- Fork pairs: shared prefix to the decision point, n=12–16 resampled continuations, judge-labelled, preferred×adversarial paired (2,843 pairs GLM / 6,391 pairs DSPARK). Reverse-DPO on the DRAFT only: `L = −log σ(β[r_w − r_l]) + α·NLL`, β=10, windowed reward (32 tokens before the committing sentence + 128-token linear-decay tail; 512 flat for run-tests). Trained: 2.15B params (GLM DFlash2 layers+norms, 0.28% of hosted); 449M (DSPARK attention+norms+hyper-connections+bigram head, 0.06%) with quantization-aware training through the deployed FP8 block format (fake-quant forward, straight-through gradient, re-quantize to deployed format).

**§5 — Evidence.**
- Held-out preference accuracy: decision frames 0.59 fwd / 0.635 adv; run-tests 0.83/0.85. **Reference preferences: decision-RIGHTNESS is unlearnable by a small draft (~chance; medians 0.52/0.46)**; thinking-length in between (0.54/0.65). The draft learns framing/behavior, not correctness — by design it never needs to.
- DeepSWE: GLM-5.3 60.6 → 67.6 (+7.0, CI 2.6–11.4); DeepSeek 67.8 → 71.8 (+4.0, CI −1.3–7.3, not significant alone). NL2Repo-Bench 65.0 → 66.4 (+1.4, p=0.011).
- **Direction control (the falsification arm the paper ships for free):** drafts trained on the same fork pairs toward the PREFERRED side land BELOW the unbiased draft (GLM 60.6 → 55.7; DeepSeek 67.8 → 66.4) — where rejections land is the mechanism, not draft quality.
- Unrelated preferences change nothing (pooled 67.5 vs 67.8, p=0.83). Acceptance cost ≤2% (expected accepted 2.28 → 2.28 GLM; 2.5 → 2.46 DeepSeek).

## 3. The crux on our stack — one decisive, cheap question

[riir-infer Plan 623](../../riir-infer/.plans/623_multi_host_pipeline_inference_lane.md) T5.1 records: *"Lossless by construction (verify is exact) — G1 byte-identity holds."* The paper forces a precise unpacking of that sentence:

- **Lossless-in-distribution** is the rejection-sampling theorem and is noise-independent — noise is just another deterministic function of the inputs.
- **Byte-identity vs serial decode** is a per-run assertion. The verify pass computes slot-s logits within a block launch (slot s attends to KV entries of slots 0..s−1 written in the SAME launch); the serial pass computes them from a single-token launch over cache-read entries. These differ **bitwise unless the kernels are batch-invariant** (the Thinking-Machines batch-invariance property: reduction order fixed regardless of launch shape). For our lane that property is **UNVERIFIED** — Bench 973's byte-exactness through a 2048-token horizon (including the G-correction arm's 95 forced rollbacks) is consistent with flip-freeness at that horizon but does not establish batch-invariance of the logits themselves. katgpt-rs [Issue 982](../.issues/982_slot_noise_census_premise_gate.md) **T0 settles this in minutes** with one bitwise compare of verify-launch vs serial-launch logits at the same logical positions; every downstream arm is conditional on its answer.

State of the measurement on our lane: [riir-ai Bench 973](../../riir-ai/.benchmarks/973_plan614_phase2_closeout_4090.md) proved the keyed-spec stream == serial keyed decode byte-exact at a **2048-token horizon**, including the G-correction arm (forced j=1 rejection every cycle, 95 real rollbacks + journal replay, byte-identical) — which is also the **exactness half of any future forced-rejection gate, already proven**. The paper's divergence regime is 30+ turns / up to 128k further tokens. The long-horizon half is **unmeasured**.

One mechanism correction (verdict round 1 — the naive reading is wrong): *"our target is Q4_K_M, a lossier weight format than the paper's NVFP4+FP8-KV+DSA stack, so our noise should be bigger"* conflates two things. **Weight quantization is deterministic and identical on both paths** — it biases neither and contributes no slot-path delta. The cross-path delta comes from launch-shape differences only: the KV write path (block-launch-written entries vs cache-read entries), reduction order inside the block launch, and batch-W-vs-1 matmul tiling. Its magnitude on our stack is **UNMEASURED** — that is the point of the census — and no above/below-the-paper prediction is claimed. What our stack uniquely contributes is the THEORY half: Issue 959's `softmax_fix.rs` (`../crates/katgpt-core/src/softmax_fix.rs`) gives closed-form per-format floors (`precision_cliff` = p·ln2 nats per accumulator format; `margin_equilibrium`) — with the honest caveat, carried into Issue 982 T3, that whether a per-accumulator cliff is the right theory for a verify-vs-decode accumulation-order delta is itself **unestablished**; the cliff probe is report-only, a theory-transfer test, never a gate.

**The decisive question, in cheap-first order:** (T0, minutes) is the verify-launch == serial-launch bitwise at the same logical positions? **Zero everywhere** → our kernels are batch-invariant for these shapes → byte-exactness is STRUCTURAL, the premise closes favorably, and the gate arm loses its motivation (an honest closure — recorded in Plan 614 + riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1). **Nonzero** → the noise channel exists; (T1) measure the cross-path delta profile by slot; if material, (T4) soak the walk at long horizon: does any keyed argmax flip occur at ≥32k tokens (128k stretch arm, ≥4 seeds)?

- **No flips at the registered bar** → the byte-exact gate's long-horizon validity is MEASURED AT THAT BAR, the noise channel stays inert in practice, the paper's mechanism stays formally inapplicable here, and the adversarial training arm stays dead behind [riir-train Issue 672](../../riir-train/.issues/672_adversarial_spec_draft_recipe_transfer.md)'s T0 trigger. The census still lands the instrument and validates the gate's premise.
- **Flips occur** → the byte-exact-vs-serial gate needs redefinition (per-step losslessness vs whole-trajectory identity are different claims — Plan 623 T5.1's sentence currently conflates them), the noise channel opens, and BOTH the paper's mechanism and the modelless placement gate become live on our stack.

This is exactly the DCOCO note's pattern (R636): **the premise gate orders FIRST, before any acceptance-lever arm.**

**Trained vs runtime gate — the honest asymmetry (advocate-merged):** the trained draft detects decision points from DRAFT-side features BEFORE the target forward (suffix draft compute saved), and catches confidently-wrong frames from framing features (entropy/margin gates are blind there; the lexicon is the modelless cover, and its recall on confident-wrong frames is the first GOAT number to produce). The runtime gate gets: zero training, zero preference data, composes with any drafter, byte-exact by the coupling theorem, a derived threshold, and the deferral upgrade — which the trained mechanism's semantics cannot express (a rejection resamples INSIDE the current block; a deferral moves the boundary).

**Advocate-finding discards (auditable reasons):** (a) the model-based advocate's "quality gain is impossible under the byte-exact gate" is KEPT but its premise is CORRECTED — the gate *asserts* byte-identity per run; it does not eliminate the bitwise verify-vs-decode delta (§3 above). (b) The no-GD advocate's in-place-resample-only semantics is WIDENED to include deferral (E8): same cost class, strictly better on the paper's own precision axis, and the ablation pair (in-place vs deferral) is pre-registered rather than chosen.

## 5. Verdict

**Pinned novelty claims (§4-precondition form):**

(a) *"Per-slot verification-noise census fused with a per-format precision cliff to derive a noise-scaled acceptance gate threshold for a keyed exact walk" — for the DFlash2-class spec lane, consuming the target's verify-pass distribution and measured slot noise, distinguished from EASD (arXiv:2512.23765)'s joint-uncertainty token-uniform numerics-blind gate by slot-position awareness, the cliff-derived threshold, and deferral semantics.*

(b) *"Decision-frame lexicon as a modelless runtime decision-point detector"* — distinguished from ConfSpec (arXiv:2602.18447) by token granularity and frame semantics; unoccupied per the §4 search.

**Tiers (per track, never pooled):**

- **Modelless track: GOAT-candidate (premise-gated)** — the fused delta survives the EASD narrowing (Q1: yes, narrowed but alive; Q3: sellable — "our acceptance gate's threshold is derived from measured per-slot path deltas against the per-format precision cliff, not hand-tuned entropy constants"; Q4: force multiplier — consumes Issue 959 numerics × the keyed walk × Plan 200 per-slot EMA). **NOT Super-GOAT: Q2 fails** — EASD already occupies the capability class ("training-free entropy rejection improves quality"); our delta is an instrument + derivation + placement improvement over that class, not a new class. The candidate stays a candidate until Issue 982's T0–T5 land: if T0 reads structural zero, the gate arm closes honestly and the residue is the instrument + the measured gate-validity record.
- **Measurement/diagnosis track: GOAT-shaped lane intel** — the census + soak is simultaneously the premise gate for the fused delta AND the threat test for the byte-exact hard gate at long horizon. File: [Issue 982](../.issues/982_slot_noise_census_premise_gate.md) (census first; the acceptance-gate arm additionally owner-gated behind the Plan-614 kernel bar — 746/747 acceptance work stays closed; the census is a numerics measurement on the existing verify path, not acceptance work).
- **Training track: deferred, secondary** (serving-envelope rule: the modelless gate runs inside the serving hot path; the training arm needs an owner-opened quality-verification mode first). Filed: [riir-train Issue 672](../../riir-train/.issues/672_adversarial_spec_draft_recipe_transfer.md) — recipe priced (~36–65 GPU-h pilot), the QAT-through-deployed-format pattern transfers to the A0 drift arm NOW (fake-quant tap simulation + bit-match G1; STE not needed for head-only training), the windowed-reward mask util is buildable zero-GPU, sequencing A0 → freeze → adversarial LoRA (never blend first — the paper's own α·NLL anchor ≈ A0, its DPO term ≈ the adversarial arm).

**MOAT gate (katgpt-rs):** in scope (speculative decode = named GOAT framing); public-safe (generic numerics + walk machinery, zero game/chain/shard/train IP); fusion consumes ≥2 shipped pillars. PASS.

**Weak point, named first:** the crux in §3 is INFERRED, not measured — the cross-path delta's existence on OUR stack follows from the paper's mechanism plus launch-shape reasoning; nobody has measured our `Δ_path(s)` (Issue 982's T0 settles existence in minutes; T1 measures the profile). If our kernels are batch-invariant for these shapes, the delta is structurally zero, the crux collapses to "structural exactness confirmed", and the gate arm loses its motivation — the design decides this cheaply either way. Second: the paper is 2 days old and unreplicated; its DeepSeek DeepSWE gain (+4.0) has a CI crossing zero and the 30-turn excess (10–20%) is borderline-significant — but the census measures OUR stack directly, so premise drift in the paper does not contaminate the instrument. Third: detector recall on confidently-wrong frames is the modelless gate's genuinely open risk (the paper's own data cuts both ways: framing learnable 0.59–0.85, rightness ~chance).

**GOAT gates (pre-registered; full arms in Issue 982):** T0 structural check — one bitwise verify-vs-serial logits compare at matched logical positions; exact-zero everywhere CLOSES the premise (structural batch-invariance; favorable verdict, recorded in Plan 614 + riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1). Census (only if T0 nonzero) — `Δ_path` slot-slope CI excludes 0 with bootstrap over (prefix × position); repeat-forward arm reads zero (determinism check — a nonzero is itself a finding: nondeterministic kernels); shuffled-slot-labels flat (instrument canary). Cliff probe — report-only, assumption-marked (never a gate). Soak — ≥32k tokens with a 128k stretch arm, ≥4 seeds, audit-mode divergence census + the re-encode isolation ablation; flips must concentrate on high-`Δ_path`/low-margin positions (separation ≥5×) or the fragility model is falsified; zero flips at the registered bar = validity MEASURED AT THAT BAR (never claimed beyond it). Gate arm (post-premise, owner-gated) — OFF / DIRECT / INVERTED / RANDOM-at-matched-rate at ≤2% throughput cost; DIRECT ≥ OFF + 1.0 pt; deferral ≥ in-place on read-back noise; DIRECT > RANDOM paired LB95 > 0.
