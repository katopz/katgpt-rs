## TL;DR

MAttr learns an **attribution ordering** over model internals (heads, neurons, edges, weight rows) by mask learning with a **differentiable sigmoid top-k** (`mask = σ((S−τ)/T)`, τ bisected so `Σmask = k` exactly) and **randomized budget k** each step — one training run supervises every sparsity, the "Matryoshka" property. It is #1 on the MIB circuit-localization leaderboard (5.6 vs 1.95 runner-up) and its RL variant localizes refusal to ~1% of the Llama-3.1-8B finetune delta (restore-to-base removes refusal, capabilities intact). For us: the mechanisms are mostly **prior-art-killed or already shipped** (our `causal_head_importance`/CNA are the modelless twins; `memory_soup` ships the weight-interpolation equation); the genuine workspace gaps are (a) the **exact-cardinality sigmoid admission operator** (`Σσ = k` — nothing ships it; `entropic_tilt` is the same bisection shape with a KL budget) and (b) the **log-frontier budget tracker** from the code (`AdaptiveLogK` — a modelless Robbins-Monro controller finding the minimal k that holds a target accuracy). Both are small katgpt-core primitives → Issue 879. No direct prefill/decode lane (attribution is quality-side; our perf league is kernel-side).

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, inference-time):**
1. `exact_mass_admit` (paper's sigmoid top-k): exact-mass soft top-k — bisection over τ ∈ [min−10T, max+10T] (≤50 iters, monotonically shrinking bracket), `mᵢ = expit((sᵢ−τ)/T)`, **sums to exactly k**; shift-invariant in S (only relative scores matter — escapes the Bilodeau completeness/linearity impossibility axioms by construction); **nested in k** (mask values monotonically increase with k). Zero-alloc bisection over a scratch bracket; house-rule-compliant (sigmoid, never softmax). **Named `exact_mass_admit` at home** — `gate_sigmoid_topk` (katgpt-spectral, Plan 279) already means hard-cut-over-sigmoid-scores; opposite mass semantics, one token apart.
2. **Log-frontier budget tracker** (from `schedules.py::AdaptiveLogK`, not in the paper text): single-scalar Robbins-Monro in log-space — track `k_max` where accuracy first hits target; sign steps `±lr` at probe_frac=0.25, log-uniform sample below the frontier. Consumes ONLY a scalar accuracy observation → **modelless-compatible** (offline calibration sweeps today; runtime quality signals later).

## 1. Paper core findings

1. **Sigmoid top-k mask** (Def 2, operator from Wijk et al. 2025 "Differentiable top-k: From one-hot to k-hot"): `SigTopK_k(S) = σ(S + c_k)` with `c_k` the bisection root of `Σσ(s_H + c_k) = k`. Implicit-diff backward: `∂ℓ/∂s_H = (σ'_H/T)·(g_H − ĝ)` where `ĝ = Σg_jσ'_j / Σσ'_j` — a **σ'-weighted centred gradient** (own grad minus weighted mean). No sparsity surrogate loss, no STE/Gumbel tricks, no L0 penalty.
2. **Matryoshka supervision**: sample `k ~ Uniform(1, N−1)` each step → learned scores are an **ordering** good at all budgets; whole CPR-vs-sparsity curve from ONE run (mask-learning baselines need ~50× compute for sparsity sweeps and still lose). Nesting is by construction (mask monotone in k).
3. **Theory** (§A): first SGD step = centred, path-reweighted **Integrated Gradients** in mask space (uniform k → `ρ(t)=6t(1−t)` Beta(2,2); log k → `2(1−t)`; logit → `1`). Adam with `ε ≫ |g|` → same (effective lr = lr/ε); Adam with tiny ε → **sign-only** updates (`Pr[d<0] − Pr[d>0]`), which *degenerates at neuron scale* — at 2.3M mask logits the repo pins `ε = 1e-2` and documents "Adam at ε≫|g| is (lr/ε)·EMA(g) = SGD with EMA momentum". Shift-invariance ⇒ not complete, not linear ⇒ Bilodeau et al. 2024 impossibility does not apply.
4. **Parameter attribution via RL** (Alg 2): interpolate weights `θ* = θ_b + α⊙(θ_s − θ_b)`, α = sigmoid top-k over per-row scores (d_model rows of each matrix = privileged basis), train with **GRPO** on a judge reward (StrongREJECT scorer). Result: restoring **1%** of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct weights to base removes refusal at ~full capability (baselines touch >27%); EG attribution fails to preserve capabilities at 1–2%. Appendix J: on-target vs off-target behavior in finetune deltas separate — ~0.1% of a delta recovers train loss; small top-k keeps on-target behavior while near-ablating off-target generalization.
5. **Code-level finds beyond the paper** (repo @ 360ff6af): `learn_scores(total, loss_fn(mask))` is genuinely environment-agnostic (universal API claim holds); k-schedules `uniform`/`log`/**`log_both`** (supervise both ends of the ordering — which nodes to keep FIRST and which to exclude LAST)/**`AdaptiveLogK`** (Robbins-Monro frontier tracker); gradient discipline — **clip-down-only, never normalize** (normalization was measured catastrophic: |s|max → 8.3e18), **float64 norm** (fp32 overflows at ~1e20 elements × (1e20)²), non-finite grad → zero the step; `k_avg` (gradient averaging over k-draws); `optimizer="none"` no-learning control = Monte-Carlo centred path-reweighted IG.

### Signal-diffs on the coverage claims (§3.6 — one read of each cousin's formula)

- **`entropic_tilt` vs `exact_mass_admit`**: entropic_tilt bisects β so `KL(q_β‖uniform) = γ` — an **information budget** on a softmax-weighted advantage vector (consumes per-group rewards, emits zero-sum advantages); `exact_mass_admit` bisects τ so `Σσ = k` — a **cardinality budget** on a keep-mask (consumes scores + budget, emits gates summing to k). Same solver pattern, different constraint object. Composable, not redundant.
- **`block_topk` vs `exact_mass_admit`**: block_topk = hard `argtopk_with_scratch` cut THEN `w = sigmoid(scores[idx])` per selected block — the cut is non-differentiable and the weights are uncalibrated (sum varies per query); `exact_mass_admit` gives every element a gate and the mass is exactly k by construction. Delta = calibration + smooth budget sweep (T knob).
- **`gate_sigmoid_topk` (katgpt-spectral) vs `exact_mass_admit`**: the shipped router gate computes independent per-expert sigmoid scores (G7 independence: perturbing one expert's row leaves others byte-identical — a deliberate anti-softmax property we share) then selection-sorts a hard top-k cut; the gate mass is uncalibrated (Σ varies per input). Ours bisects the threshold so the mass is exactly k and the mask is nested in k — a complement, not a replacement: same independence semantics, calibrated budget added. The `_into` out-param shape is the API pattern to follow.
- **`memory_soup` vs MAttr-parameters**: soup gates interpolate between **checkpoints** per-query (`γᵢ = σ(dot(W_u·q, reprᵢ)/√d)`, gate projection trained by backprop); MAttr learns a **static per-parameter-row** α by RL on a behavioral judge. Different signal (query-conditioned blend vs global causal localization), same interpolation equation.
- **Healer `necessity.rs` vs MAttr ordering**: necessity-bench measures per-rule Δflip-rate at full corpus (per-decision pricing); MAttr optimizes prefixes of an ordering jointly over budgets. The `log_both` schedule (exclude-last supervision) is the demotion-question shape (Issue 102), but the healer's evidence accumulation (Beta/Elo/LCB) is already outcome-labeled — the delta is budget-joint optimization only. No new issue beyond 133.
