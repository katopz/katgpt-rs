## TL;DR

Looped transformers wired carry-last (`h_t = z_t`) suffer **latent overthinking**: later iterations overwrite correct intermediate deductions in the recurrent state — TRM's Sudoku accuracy peaks at 62.2% (468 effective layers) and falls to 58.1% (1,872); 20.9% of correct cell predictions get overwritten at least once after update 36; solved puzzles decay back to churn. **InfiLoop replaces carry-last with a normalized running summary**: content score `s_i = q^⊤ RMSNorm(z_i)` (one shared pseudo-query) × learned decay `exp(−λ(t−i))`, admitting an **exact O(d) streaming recurrence** `N_t = βN_{t−1} + e_t z_t`, `D_t = βD_{t−1} + e_t`, `h_t = N_t/D_t` (`β = e^{−λ}`, `e_t = exp(s_t)`) — memory constant in the loop count, equivalent to an adaptive gated update `h_t = h_{t−1} + γ_t(z_t − h_{t−1})` with `γ_t = e_t/(βD_{t−1}+e_t) ∈ (0,1)`. The state stays in the **convex hull** of candidates (`‖h_t‖ ≤ max‖z_i‖`), solved states survive 10⁻⁴–0.3 perturbations at 100% retention (TRM loses 15–20% even unperturbed), and accuracy scales monotonically past **20,000 effective steps**. 7M params: Sudoku-Extreme 97.9 (FPRM 94.2, TRM 74.7), ARC-AGI-2 13.6 pass@2 (TRM 7.8).

**The workspace-relevant identity (ours, not the paper's):** `γ_t = e_t/(βD_{t−1}+e_t) = σ(s_t − ln(βD_{t−1}))` — the adaptive step is **exactly a sigmoid of the log-ratio of new-content score to decayed accumulated mass**. The full softmax over candidates (Eq. 2) never needs to exist: a scalar sigmoid + two accumulators reproduce the aggregation exactly. This makes the mechanism **sigmoid-law compliant** (AGENTS.md: sigmoid, never softmax) and modelless-usable the moment a per-write quality score exists — the trained `q` is replaceable by any shipped scorer (observation confidence, coherence, cadence shape).

**Modelless extraction (the algebra needs no training to USE):**
1. **N/D gated-summary kernel** — two accumulators + one sigmoid per write; zero-alloc, O(d), deterministic. The primitive: `GatedSummary<const D> { n: [f32; D], mass: f32, beta: f32 }` with `step(z, score)` — **β=1 must reproduce the shipped `PrecisionVector` update exactly (the G1 oracle); the new content is β-decay + content-scored weights**, never a fork of the pruner substrate.
2. **Sigmoid write gate** `γ = σ(score − ln(β·D))` — evidence-mass-weighted write acceptance: accumulated solid evidence resists single noisy overwrites in exact proportion to its decayed mass; a fresh accumulator accepts the first solid write fully. Edge: at the first write `D=0` so `ln 0 = −∞` ⇒ γ=1 exactly — the t=1 case is pinned as a test, not left to log-domain luck.
3. **Convex-hull boundedness** — standing invariant: the summary never amplifies beyond the largest candidate, never decays to zero (unlike leaky EMA), never overflows (the paper's cure for residual growth under weight sharing).
4. **Forward-decay config law** — β ∈ (0,1) is load-bearing: reverse decay (β>1) scores **2.0%**, uniform (β=1) 38.4%, forward (β=0.55) 74.0%; learned-β from β₀=0.55 best (82.1/89.7). β₀=0.10 → 66.3, β₀=0.90 → 63.3 — the middle of the range is the regime.
5. **Log-domain numerics** — clip `s` (paper clips ln e_t to ±8); the two-accumulator form is finite-precision-safe when scores are clipped (no softmax denominator blow-up).
6. **Dual-axis placement law** — in nested loops, gate BOTH axes (82.1 vs inner-only 72.6 / outer-only 75.1; margin widens with budget: 89.7 vs 79.4/80.0 at H32).
7. **Churn-prevention as the detector's missing half** — `ConvergenceCadence`/`LoopResidualExit` *detect* post-solution churn (Issue 720/731); the gated write *prevents* the overwrite-driven class. Detector + preventer compose.
8. **Content-score ablation bound** — removing the score costs 6–7 pts (q=0 → 84.1/84.8 vs 90.9); the content gate is not "damping with extra steps" (a fixed EMA also damps and scores less). Any modelless transplant must carry a real per-write score, not a constant.

**Training track (riir-train Plan 465):** the InfiLoop connection (d+1 params per axis: `q ∈ R^d`, `ln λ`) as an amendment arm on the looped-solver line — Plan 373's late-state looped solver measured an **Anderson-divergence NEGATIVE**, and InfiLoop's convex-hull-bounded normalized aggregation is the exact anti-divergence shape (bounded amplitude, perturbation damping, no fixed-point solver to diverge); Plan 459's oscillatory damping probe gains a write-gate axis that sidesteps the QRR tension law (gate the write, don't damp the settling oscillation).

**Verdict: GAIN, not Super-GOAT.** The diagnosis and even the term are published (Bansal 2022 `2202.05826` coined overthinking-with-recall; Think-at-Hard `2511.08577` used "latent overthinking" for looped transformers in Nov 2025; STARS `2605.26733` documented peak-then-collapse); the aggregation math is a clean re-composition of published pieces (RetNet/linear-attention decayed streaming + AttnRes content scoring + ACT normalized ponder aggregation) transplanted onto the loop axis. Our claims are "first workspace consumer" + "modelless extraction of the gate algebra + the first preventer for the churn the cadence family detects" — GOAT-shaped engineering claims, never novelty claims. ## 1. Paper core findings

1. **Latent overthinking (the diagnosis).** Under carry-last, TRM on Sudoku-Extreme: peak 62.2% @ 468 layers → 58.1% @ 1,872; updates 36–72 change 77.0 predicted cells/puzzle on empty cells (InfiLoop 24.0), +0.21 incorrect cells/puzzle (InfiLoop −1.25); **20.9% of correct predictions at update 36 get overwritten at least once** (InfiLoop 2.4%); after first solve, TRM's per-update state change falls to 3% then climbs back to 10% and stays — it begins losing solved puzzles after 9 updates and has lost 19.7% after 36. InfiLoop: 0.3%/update, every solved puzzle still solved 36 updates later. Once an error propagates long-range through the recurrence, later loops cannot correct it.
2. **The connection (Eq. 2).** `h_t = Σ_{i≤t} α_{t,i} z_i`, `α_{t,i} = exp(s_i − λ(t−i)) / Σ_j exp(s_j − λ(t−j))`, `s_i = q^⊤ RMSNorm(z_i)` — one learned pseudo-query shared across iterations (scored once, when produced), learned decay λ > 0.
3. **Exact streaming (Eq. 4, the load-bearing trick).** With `e_i = exp(s_i)`, `β = e^{−λ}`: `N_t = βN_{t−1} + e_t z_t`, `D_t = βD_{t−1} + e_t`, `h_t = N_t/D_t`. Algebraically identical to Eq. 2, no approximation. O(d) persistent memory per token per axis (582.8 KiB/puzzle total, constant in loop count); total aggregation cost O(Td) vs O(T²d) for full Attention Residuals; O(Td) vs O(Td) memory growth for AttnRes.
4. **Adaptive-residual reading (Eq. 5).** `h_t = h_{t−1} + γ_t(z_t − h_{t−1})`, `γ_t = e_t/(βD_{t−1}+e_t)` — compares the candidate's content weight against the retained historical mass. Convex weights ⇒ `h_t` in the convex hull of candidates ⇒ `‖h_t‖ ≤ max‖z_i‖` (norm bound uniform in t once candidate norms are bounded).
5. **Emergent self-verification (trained-model behavior).** γ_t ≈ 0.40 during search (< the equal-weight baseline 1−β = 0.56), surges to 0.91 at the solving update, settles to 0.56 within 6 updates; unsolved puzzles never surge. Content score tracks decoded-board correctness **without supervision**: median ln e_t = 7.25 (correct) → 2.75 (20 wrong) → −0.58 (33 wrong).
6. **Stability under perturbation.** Relative perturbation ε ∈ [10⁻⁴, 0.3] injected at update 36: InfiLoop retains **100%** of solved puzzles at update 72 at every scale; TRM loses 15–20% even unperturbed; TRM's trajectory diverges to O(1) relative distance within 12 updates from ε = 10⁻⁴ alone (chaotic drift), InfiLoop's displacement stays bounded.
7. **Ablations.** Dual-axis > single-axis (82.1 vs 72.6/75.1 at training depth; 89.7 vs 79.4/80.0 at H32). Forward decay critical (§TL;DR row 4). Content score worth 6–7 pts both when trained-without and when removed-at-test. β=0 at test → 1.5% (the block is trained to read the aggregated state, not raw outputs — the connection is load-bearing, not a bolt-on).
8. **Results (7M).** Sudoku-Extreme **97.9** exact (FPRM 94.2, EqR 93.0, TRM 74.7, HRM-27M 55.0; frontier LLMs 0.0); Maze-Hard 87.4; ARC-AGI-1 49.1 pass@2; ARC-AGI-2 **13.6** (TRM 7.8, FPRM 6.2, Gemini 2.5 Pro 4.9). Test-time scaling: 90.9% at 1,872 layers = FPRM's 90.1% at 3,744 (**half the compute**); monotone improvement through 24,960 layers (92.74%) and >20,000 effective steps.

## 4. Modelless distillation

### 4.1 The sigmoid identity (the key to the modelless transplant)

`γ_t = e_t/(βD_{t−1}+e_t)`. Divide through by `e_t`: `γ_t = 1/(1 + βD_{t−1}/e_t) = 1/(1 + exp(ln(βD_{t−1}) − s_t)) = σ(s_t − ln(βD_{t−1}))`. The softmax over the full candidate history (Eq. 2) **never needs to exist** — a scalar sigmoid of the log-ratio plus two accumulators reproduces the aggregation exactly. This is why the mechanism is modelless-usable: the trained pseudo-query `q` defines a *scorer*; any shipped scorer defines it just as well. Candidates on this stack: fog-of-war observation confidence (two-brain `visible_radius` gating), coherence scores (`reestimation` family), cadence shapes, `quality_gate` separation ratios, runetrace quality. Calibration is the shipped sigmoid-calibration discipline (the `SigmoidGateCalibrator` posture), not a new training run. Score clipping at ±8 (the paper's own convention) keeps `exp` finite without a softmax denominator.

### 4.2 The gated-summary kernel (Issue 950 P2–P3)

`SummaryAccum { n: [f32; D], mass: f32 }`, `step(z, s)`: `e = exp(clip(s, ±8))`; `n = β·n + e·z`; `mass = β·mass + e`; read `h = n/mass`. Zero-alloc, fixed-D, deterministic, f32 throughout (two FMA chains + one exp + one div per write). The gate form `γ = σ(s − ln(β·mass))` is available for consumers that want the *step* view (belief updates) without the *mean* view. Properties by construction: convex-hull boundedness (test), no decay-to-zero (test vs leaky), monotone mass growth under sustained writes, forward-decay β ∈ (0,1) asserted at construction (the 2.0% reverse-decay catastrophe is a config error, refuse loud).

### 4.3 Why the workspace lacks exactly this (the honest gap statement — verdict-round-1 correction)

Every ingredient ships — **including the undecayed gate itself**: `PrecisionVector` (`katgpt-pruners/src/posterior/precision.rs`) runs `mean += (obs − mean)·(w/(precision_old + w))`, which is InfiLoop's `γ` with β=1 and a fixed caller weight `w`; `posterior/surprise.rs` adds a sigmoid-gated surprise read on the same state. The prior-art sweep missed it because "precision-weighted posterior / Kalman gain / BAKE" was never in the grep vocabulary — the R296 vocabulary-translation failure, caught by the verdict reviewer, recorded here as the standing lesson.

What does **not** ship anywhere is the composition of four deltas over that shipped base:
1. **Forward decay on the mass counter** (β<1 forgetting — `PrecisionVector` only grows; the paper's 2.0/38.4/74.0 ablation says this axis is load-bearing, not cosmetic);
2. **Content-scored weights** (`e = exp(score(z))` derived from the observation itself, not a caller constant — the paper's q=0 ablation: −6.8 pts, so the content signal is the point, and a constant-weight arm is the shipped posture);
3. **The loop-iteration + belief-tick axis** (`forward_looped` re-entry, NPC perception writes — the surfaces the paper diagnoses as carry-last);
4. **The convex-hull invariant as a tested law** (`‖h‖ ≤ max‖z_i‖`, plus never-decays-to-zero — untested/undocumented on the precision state).

The consequence is measurable in shipped behavior: `evolve_belief` overwrites on every write (one noisy perception moves an established belief; Issue 934's clamped cumulative variant pins 6 of 8 dims within a few dozen calls for ANY stream), and the looped-forward surfaces (`forward_looped`, `loop_probe`'s re-entry) are carry-last — the exact posture the paper diagnoses. The primitive would *prevent* what the cadence family can only *detect* — by adding decay + content-scoring to the gated mean the workspace already trusts.

### 5. Fusion (the novel combination — paper × 623 × 529)

1. **Evidence-gated belief commit (priority #1 shape).** The phase_belief substrate (Issue 939: `z ← ρR(θ)z + u`, fixed rotation carry) gains TWO commit signals instead of one: the **magnitude residual** `r_free = ‖u‖/(‖z‖+ε)` (623 §4.3 — writes stopped?) and the **content gate** `γ = σ(score − ln(βD))` (this paper — writes worth accepting?). A belief commits when writes stop *and* recent writes were low-quality; it flips when a high-quality write arrives with force. Neither signal alone decides: a damped rotating channel never settles in magnitude (623's own P2 ambiguity), and content without magnitude lets tiny writes flip beliefs.
2. **Detector + preventer loop hygiene.** `ConvergenceCadence` (529/720) reads churn; the gated write removes the overwrite-driven churn class. Composition: cadence verdict feeds the gate as a soft score input (churning loop → accepted write weight drops → the loop can settle), and the cadence then verifies the settle. Never a bypass — the gate is data, the FSM/cadence stay the deciders (the Brain+FSM law).
3. **GDN family closure.** Research 070's "Ternary-Bonsai is scalar-decay GDN" + Research 538's decay-overwrite noise control + this paper's loop-axis N/D summary = one algebra (`S ← βS + gated write`, normalized readout optional) running over three axes: tokens (GDN/league model), loop iterations (this paper), and evidence ticks (belief kernels). The workspace already runs it twice; the third axis is a transplant, not a new mechanism.
4. **What none alone has:** a per-NPC latent belief that is simultaneously rotation-carrying (939), commit-law-gated in magnitude (623), evidence-mass-gated in content (this paper), churn-verified (529/720), and bounded by construction (convex hull) — with O(1) per-tick cost on the 20 Hz path.

## 6. Game-context reframe (priority #1)

Per-NPC belief channels in the two-brain model are **unconditioned writes** today: `leaky_step` moves the belief by `lr·(input share)` on every perception, and one noisy observation flips an established belief (the stochastic flip-flop the frustration drives currently detect post-hoc). The InfiLoop transplant: **belief-update acceptance weighted by evidence quality relative to accumulated evidence mass** — a monster observed 50 times under good visibility resists one fog-corrupted repositioning in exact proportion to its decayed evidence; a fresh belief accepts the first solid observation fully (no fixed patience counter, a sigmoid drive over a measured quantity, per the Brain+FSM law). The summary never fades to zero when observations stop (unlike the leaky EMA) — the belief *persists* at its summary value while the two-brain's time-based `decay_confidence` handles "how stale", an orthogonal axis. Post-goal churn (the FSM oscillation pathology, 623 §4.4): the gated write removes the overwrite-driven class; the cadence verifies the settle. Real-boot PROGRESS-test convention and defer-until-pull posture follow Issue 939.

## 7. Healer-context reframe (priority #2, honest)

The healer has no deep iterative latent loop (same verdict as 623 §4.5). The one honest surface: fix-candidate scoring across a heal run could adopt the N/D read — candidates scored over successive proposer passes with oracle-verdict-weighted, decayed aggregation (a convex mean of evidence rather than the current argmax-ish selection) — but `select_best_candidate`'s signal composition is a different shape and no churn pathology is documented there.
