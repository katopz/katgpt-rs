## TL;DR

A Blackwell-FP4 attention kernel paper with three transferable *ideas* and one high-value *datum*. **Ideas:** (1) **Direct-P** — treat softmax on a quantized consumer as a *code-classification* problem: fit a 2-constant affine map `u(x)=max(0, Ax+B)` directly to the E2M1 decision boundaries instead of computing an accurate exponential and rounding it away; (2) the **represented-denominator contract** — accumulate the softmax normalizer from the *same* rounded codes the PV product consumes, so numerator and denominator describe one approximate operator; (3) pass the **forward quantization into backward** (save payload + scales + LSE; reconstruct P exactly w.r.t. the quantized payload — published prior art: this is Attn-QAT 2603.00040's own principle 1). **Datum:** a four-arm factorial (E4M3/NVFP4 projections × FP8/MXFP4 P/V) where **both MXFP4-P/V arms diverge ~0.1B tokens in while both FP8-P/V arms descend 55.5B tokens** — P/V format is the isolated separator, independent of projection format. **Verdict: PASS** on the kernel body (the fleet has no FP4 tensor cores and no quantized-P consumer anywhere; every mechanism is published here first with in-paper lineage), with the stability datum + recipe items recorded for the deferred QAT plans and the kernel lessons queued as a distill candidate.

## 1. Paper core findings (verified against the fetched full text)

1. **Direct-P forward.** Score→probability becomes code classification: `x = (z−m)·log2 e − eB + log2 6`, `q = QE2M1(max(0, Ax+B))`, with the transform, block exponent, and log2 6 folded into packed-FMA coefficients. Calibrated pairs: fast **(A=1.50, B=1.20)**, accurate **(A=1.60, B=0.95)**; these move the seven E2M1 decision boundaries — "quantizer-calibration parameters, not changes to exact softmax." Selective native EX2 on ~25% of pair positions (accurate policy; all-affine fast on GB200). Up to **2.13× BF16** forward on GB200 D128 (peak 2998–3159 TFLOP/s); cosine **0.944** (fast) / 0.952 (accurate) vs HAO's FP8-P/V at **0.9899** — a speed-for-accuracy trade, honestly reported.
2. **Represented denominator.** The normalizer is summed from the E2M1 payload codes + E8M0 block scales *as consumed by PV* (block code sums via DP4A byte reduction): `d = α·(Σc/6)`. Numerator and denominator describe one approximate operator — no normalizing an FP4 numerator with an unrelated FP32 exponential sum.
3. **Sampled-anchor guard.** For extreme-logit layers: 128 globally-distributed K/V rows permuted into the first key tile offline (exactness-preserving); row reference `mi = ai + M·ln2` with compile-time `M + L ≤ 126`; the working E8M0 byte is floored at `L+1`; the denominator reassociates as `α·(Σc/6)`, **never** `(α/6)·Σc` — at E8M0 code 1 the first form is subnormal and flushes a valid block to zero (the original Wan layer-39 failure: the sampled anchor equaled the exact max; the failure was *solely* the subnormal intermediate).
4. **Causal training path.** Forward saves NVFP4 Q/K payload bytes, block scales, per-head global factors, and per-row LSE; backward recomputes QKᵀ from those bytes ("exact" = w.r.t. the quantized forward payload) and reconstructs P via LSE — no separate BF16 score path. Gradient products in FP8: **E4M3 operands, E5M2 for dO** — E4M3 rounded **~97% of observed dO values to zero**; E5M2 ~14% at 0.7–0.9% publisher cost. Two independent **factor-of-256** bugs documented (missing `+8` LSE lift → P at 1/256 scale; the gradient epilogue's own 1/256 correction that must NOT be folded into the LSE fix). 2D **row-by-K16** Q/K scales cut pre-clip grad norms **~20,000 → 161–184** (a fixed-head rule failed immediately at 8B — scale granularity is shape-sensitive).
5. **Measured training economics.** Isolated backward 1.405×; projection-inclusive fwd+bwd 1.245×; complete 8B update up to **1.14×** at B4 (speedup grows with batch saturation: 1.085× B1 → 1.137× B4). Matched 64-GPU 100B-token run: **1.112×** sustained throughput with a **+0.090** validation-loss gap at 99.99B tokens — both axes reported together.
6. **The four-arm factorial (the headline datum).** E4M3/NVFP4 learned projections × FP8/MXFP4 attention P/V, backward held fixed: **both MXFP4-P/V arms separate from their FP8 controls near 0.1B tokens** (loss 7.97 vs 5.41 at update 500; pre-clip grad norms into the millions) under BOTH projection formats; both FP8-P/V arms stay non-divergent through 55.5B tokens; an independent B4 arm (E4M3 projections + MXFP4 P/V) reproduced the explosion. Forward MXFP4 P/V — or the model state it induces — is the common separator. MXFP4-V-in-backward was separately rejected on cost (dual publication ate the saving; shared-tile producer too slow; approximate consumer perturbed dQ/dK).
7. **Hardware takeaways (measured).** TMEM's 512 columns are fully owned by 2 FP32 score banks + 2 FP32 output banks at D128 → no allocatable third score destination; low tensor-pipe activity (18.8% with real P construction, same 98,304 tensor instructions) is a **readiness-stall symptom**, not arithmetic shortage; speed-of-light probes bound the remainder — removing nearly all probability construction saves only **5.23%** on the final kernel; B300's doubled SFU rate does not scale the whole loop; accelerating one N32 producer quarter does not advance a K64 pair command.
8. **Calibration composability negative.** Layer-wise affine maps optimized on a BF16 teacher trajectory did NOT predict the composed all-FP4 trajectory — favorable single-layer changes reversed sign on fresh prompts. Rejected as production calibration; requires fresh-process, unseen-prompt validation.
9. **UE5M3 pointer.** Companion paper: unsigned E5M3 block scales (wider range than E8M0's power-of-two) enable stable FP4 pretraining — the proposed route to full-FP4 attention training. External search confirms a dense published landscape: 2509.25149 (NVFP4 pretraining), 2509.17791 (UE5M3 > MXFP4), 2605.09825 (MXFP4 stability hypotheses), 2601.19026 (microscaling limits), and **Attn-QAT 2603.00040**, whose stated principle 1 ("matching low-precision recomputation of attention scores in the backward pass") *is* this paper's training-path idea.

## 3. Three-track adversarial panel (merged)

- **No-GD advocate (18-item ledger, all training-free):** strongest extractions are the code-agreement fitting method, the represented-denominator contract, the reassociation law, the sampled-anchor guard, and the SoL/readiness methodology — each with a concrete falsifiable gate design. Meta-verdict: "the entire paper's guard/schedule/constant surface is modelless-extractable; its measured numbers transfer as GOAT-gate reference targets." Coverage pass (this section's table) found **no firing site for any of them** in the current tree — the extraction surface is real but consumerless.
- **Model-based advocate (12-item ledger):** the durable recipes for the deferred QAT/quant-robust-LoRA plans — (1) recompute-parity: backward must differentiate the *quantized* op the forward executed, verified by bit-comparing forward vs backward-recomputed scores at the same payload; (2) per-operand gradient format table chosen by measured zero-flush rate (the 97%→14% dO datum is the calibration method); (3) stage-wise error attribution — quantize projections first, keep the attention kernel high-precision (their isolation: 0.3614 projection-borne vs 0.0216 kernel-added relative-L2); (4) the P/V floor doctrine: any P/V-quantization proposal needs a ≥2-arm factorial with divergence detection before landing; (5) matched-run accounting — throughput AND validation gap together, or the bench is invalid by construction (matches our Issue-750-T3 lossy-surface rule); (6) batch-saturation caveat — B4 numbers are inadmissible for B1-shaped workloads (our LoRA steps are exactly that shape).

## 4. Latent-space / game-context / consumer-context reframes (mandatory steps)

- **Latent reframe (the honest conceptual tie):** Direct-P is the **decode-at-boundary principle applied to softmax on a tensor core** — "don't compute precision you are about to round away." Same family as the scale-deferred GEMM refutation (Bench 773), delayed-RMS scale (B29), and the house latent-to-latent / decode-only-at-boundary rule. The paper is external evidence that the principle pays when the consumer is a code lattice and the intermediate is off the critical path.
- **Game-context:** none. No per-NPC scalar is bounded; the 20 Hz cognition path runs Bonsai ternary on M3 Metal / 4090 CUDA — no FP4 silicon, no softmax-code consumer. Recorded rather than forced.
- **Consumer-context (healer):** the consuming surface is the **kernel_opt corpus** (the healer's GPU-kernel rule domain): candidate rules with decision content include *low-tensor-activity-is-a-readiness-symptom* (diagnosis rule), *per-fragment optimization below the consumer's issue granularity is invisible* (K64-pair law), and the reassociation law. Expected yield: riders + 1–2 rules — Blackwell-TMEM specifics have no analog in the Metal/CUDA fleet, and B79/B80/B108 already cover the readiness/dispatch territory. Queued as a candidate, not scheduled.
