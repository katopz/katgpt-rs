## TL;DR

EBR replaces the learned recurrent transition of HRM/TRM with **gradient descent on a learned scalar energy** E(z;x) over latent reasoning states — one function that simultaneously supplies the refinement direction (−∇E), the stopping signal (‖∇E‖ < τ or a windowed state residual), and candidate selection (argmin E over K independent descent trajectories, no separate verifier). Training adds a **convergence-supervision loss** — BCE on a sigmoid of the state residual against prediction correctness — forcing "converged ⇒ correct" so wrong fixed points keep moving; the paper's single largest ablation (removing it: 98.96% → 45.32% Sudoku single-sample). Multi-solution diversity comes from SXM (state-based Explorative Modeling): K parallel trajectories, supervise only the best-matched, solution non-reuse prevents collapse.

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, inference-time):** every RUNTIME mechanism in this paper is already occupied territory in this workspace — residual halting ships as `GainCostLoopHalter` + `ConvergenceCadence` (Issue 720 / Research 529), best-of-K selection ships as `select_best_belief` / `BoMSampler`+`BanditPruner` / `seat_rerank`, wrong-attractor detection ships as `depth_invariance` + the cadence's plateau-high→fail semantics, and "one scalar drives replan/give-up/choose" is the standing sigmoid-drive doctrine. The paper's modelless residue is **corroboration + one law condition made explicit**: (i) EBR computes residuals on **unit-RMS-pinned states**, which is precisely the condition under which relative residuals are valid — the existing halt-signal law (Bench 699 §T6 / the `gain_cost_halt.rs` doc warning) gains its boundary condition stated in the code doc ("relative residual valid only on norm-pinned states"; the halter's `angular_change` already shipped the scale-free direction form); (ii) the "converged ≠ correct" discipline is independently confirmed at benchmark scale; (iii) the constraint-count energy framing ("a fixer loop IS an energy-based reasoner": E = outstanding diagnostics, ∇E = per-lint deltas, selection = argmin over drafts) is exact vocabulary for what `fix_verify`'s compile gate already does — occupied, no filing. The **open deltas are training-side** and route to riir-train beside Plan 373 (Issue 682): the convergence-supervision loss (rank 1, with loss-side RMS normalization — the free-norm relative residual is forbidden here), a detach-boundary/window ablation (rank 2, a Plan 373-native extension — EBR's H=7 is a cost cut, not a mechanism credit), noisy-solution init, randomized descent schedules, and the full energy lane sequenced behind a falsification rule.

## 1. Paper Core Findings

1. **Energy-descent recurrence.** Latent state z ∈ R^{N×d} (one vector per token); backbone transformer f computes per-token energies summed into E(z;x) = Σᵢ φ(SiLU(W hᵢ + b)·w); update z ← Π_M(z̃ − α∇E + noise) with Π_M = per-token projection to unit RMS (sphere). Gradient coupling flows through attention — per-token energy terms, coupled refinement.
2. **Adaptive stopping without a halt head.** Stop when ‖∇_M E‖ < τ (τ=0.2) or after S segments; also works with the residual r = windowed mean relative change over the last H steps. 10.8× inference-FLOP saving at equal accuracy (99.9% Sudoku-Extreme); 345× fewer FLOPs than EqR at comparable accuracy. Residuals computed on NORMALIZED states.
3. **Energy-based selection ≈ oracle.** Among N independent trajectories, picking the lowest-energy endpoint near-matches test-label oracle selection and beats picking the most-converged endpoint (fig. 7).
4. **Convergence supervision** (largest ablation): p = σ((δ−r)/κ), L_conv = BCE(p, 1[final correct]); removing it: 98.96 → 45.32; replacing it with TRM's Q-head: 92.42 (the loss beats the learned halt head at equal parameter budget).
5. **SXM** (state-based Explorative Modeling, extending XM arXiv:2607.27372 to descent trajectories): K=2 trajectories per input; per segment only the best-matched trajectory is supervised (forward: trajectory→target; reverse: target→closest not-yet-used valid solution, non-reuse until 75% assigned); unselected trajectories keep evolving. Coverage on 10×10 N-Queens: 57.5% → 92.0%. Partial solution sets (k≥2 targets) recover most of the reverse-SXM gain.
6. **Randomized descent** (per-segment n∈U(15,21), α∈U(200,250), noisy-solution init z₀ = mix of noise and a solution-decoding state with fraction a~U(0,1)) — ablation: fixing α costs ~1.4pp.
7. **Energy head cost**: +66K params at d=256 (~3.7%); second-order gradients through descent (truncated to final H=7 states/segment); 1.8M params total on Sudoku — smallest model in its comparison table while beating HRM (27M), TRM (7M), GRAM (10M), Looped Flows (5–7M).

### 2.2 Published prior art (web sweep, §4)

The class is established — by the paper's own lineage: IREM (Du, ICML 2022), IRED (2406.11179, diffusion-annealed energy descent + difficulty-adaptive step count), EBT (2507.02092, ICLR 2026 oral — energy minimization "until convergence", the model IS the verifier), Energy Transformer (2302.07253, associative-memory ancestor), Hyper-SET (2502.11646). Fast-follows a Nov-2026 submission must be read against: FPRM (2606.18206 — closest single prior for residual halting, unsupervised), ERM (2607.10128 — Hopfield-energy selection over recursive computation), EBM-CoT (2511.07124), Metacognitive EBM (2610.00399), EBT-Policy (2510.27545), CEM (2510.20607). **CRAM (2611.17309, 2026-11-12) — distilled as Research 670**: the no-BPTT local-objective (PL) counterpoint on a tabular pairwise energy; its fitted-LEVEL stop (measured 0% wrong-halts vs 0.05–3.54% for this paper family's halting rules) is the runtime delta this census does not cover — its descent-search deltas (K chains, abstain-on-budget) extend `katgpt-assign` (607/620), not a new driver; training rows on riir-train Issue 682. Halting class prior art: ACT (1603.08983), PonderNet (2107.05407), Change of Thought (2507.13569), answer-convergence early stopping (EMNLP 2025). Diversity-training prior art: XM (2607.27372 — SXM's direct parent), IMLE (1809.09087), CHIMLE, DDN (ICLR 2025 — the non-reuse ancestry), plus a critical analysis (2608.05423).

**Per-mechanism verdicts:** M1 energy-descent recurrence / M2 residual halting / M3 energy-based selection = PRIOR ART EXISTS (strong). M4 convergence-supervision loss and M5 SXM = PARTIAL — components exist (FPRM unsupervised halting; PonderNet supervised halt-head; XM in data space), the exact couplings (BCE-supervised state-residual convergence; XM transplanted into latent descent states) not found published. The full triple (supervised-convergence halting + energy selection + XM-trained states) has no direct hit; every pairwise combination does.

### 2.3 Fusion (the two-sided standing check)

- **Rulewright:** the fixer's `fix_verify` loop already implements the constraint-count energy framing end to end — E = outstanding diagnostics (the compile gate), descent = apply fixes, wrong-attractor rejection = auto-REVERT, stopping = refusal taxonomy incl. `loop_exhausted`, outcome-coupling MEASUREMENT = `fixseq` AUC(k) (lead-k decodability of attempt outcomes). EBR adds the TRAIN-the-coupling half — which requires a trained looped model the fixer does not have (corpus-is-the-model). No runtime filing.
- **Decision-serving (ESC + reflex):** the ESC stack already runs the full semantics — rate guard (escalation budget), VoI readout (value-per-escalation), audit latch (Wilson-bounded demote/recover), G0 falsifier (supervision collapse onto cheap confidence = the wrong-fixed-point veto, shipped). The ESC gate is EXACT/stationary (no mutated state), so EBR's convergence-score readout fails the stationary-surface test there. The cheap/think escalation ladder IS the "converged but wrong ⇒ keep moving" law in production. No filing.
- **Game runtime:** `HuntStalemateTrack` (issue 1175) already ships the σ-shaped frustration drive over MEASURED stasis, folding salience so the FSM replans — the paper's wrong-fixed-point discipline in game vocabulary; `ConvergenceCadence`→escalate (720 T3) ships the halt semantics; `depth_invariance` ships collapse detection. Corroboration only.

### 2.4 The law refinement (modelless residue, ADOPTED in the same commit)

**Normalized-state residual boundary condition — makes an existing law's condition explicit.** The working form of the halt-signal law already lives as the "Halt-signal warning" doc on `crates/katgpt-core/src/gain_cost_halt.rs` (never use `‖F(h)−h‖/‖h‖` on non-fixed-point recurrences; Bench 699 §T6, line 144, records the measured basis: sotaku state RMS 24→706 while the absolute residual plateaued ≈0.63 — the relative form fell to median 0.000894 as a pure growing-denominator artifact, and Anderson/Broyden solvers driven by it diverged to norms 1e5–1e8). The halter also already ships `angular_change` — a scale-free direction signal — so a norm-invariant form was not wholly absent; what EBR's practice ADDS is the boundary condition stated explicitly: project to constant norm every step (Π_M) ⇒ ‖z_t‖ constant ⇒ relative and scaled-absolute coincide ⇒ the statistic is monotone-behaved. **Law, now stated in the code doc (qualifier added to gain_cost_halt.rs in this filing's commit): a relative residual is a valid convergence signal only on norm-pinned states; on free-norm states use absolute windowed Δ (cadence) or the direction form (angular_change).** No current looped lane pins state norms, so no behavior change — the qualifier arms the first lane that does (Plan 373's f16-state precision arm and Issue 682 arm 1's loss-side normalization are the natural readers).

### 2.5 Closest cousins (why they don't kill the deltas)

1. **Research 049 (PTRM)** — noise + K rollouts + Q-head halting: inference-side mechanisms overlap heavily, and PTRM's filed NOT-to-do list explicitly rejects a separate trained halt head ("online bandit strictly better") — but PTRM keeps a LEARNED TRANSITION and supervises all rollouts against one target; EBR's deltas (gradient-from-scalar-field dynamics, trained converged⇒correct coupling, trajectory-matched supervision) are all outside PTRM's recorded scope.
2. **Research 035 (Attractor Models)** — fixed-point refinement with residual-based exit + IFT backward: the halting-statistics family and the one-step-backward alternative; EBR's L_conv (training the coupling) and SXM (diversity) are absent, and 035's solver-on-1D-relevance disproof (Plan 053) targets a surface EBR never touches.
3. **Research 529 / Issue 720 cadence** — the runtime detector + escalate semantics; EBR corroborates its premise (solved ⇒ converging, failed ⇒ plateau) and adds the training half that makes the premise true by construction.
4. **riir-train Plan 373 (sotaku)** — the looped-training lane; EBR's recipe arms EXTEND it (Issue 682), they do not collide: 373's G1/G2 gates are the proof vehicle; the detach-boundary/window ablation is 373-native (gradients crossing the burn-in→tail seam on long-horizon tiers), with EBR's H=7 cited only as the cost-cut precedent.

## 3. Verdict

**Pinned novelty claim (§1.5 precondition form):** for the workspace's looped-reasoner distillation family, the paper's defensible deltas are (M4) a correctness-BCE-supervised convergence loss over a state-residual sigmoid and (M5) SXM trajectory-matched supervision with solution non-reuse — while every runtime mechanism (residual halting, best-of-K selection, unified scalar drives, wrong-attractor detection) is prior-art-covered published-side (IREM/IRED/EBT/FPRM lineage) and occupied workspace-side (`GainCostLoopHalter`/`ConvergenceCadence`/`select_best_belief`/`depth_invariance`).
