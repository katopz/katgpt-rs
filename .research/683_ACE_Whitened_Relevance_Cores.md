## TL;DR

ACE (Algorithmic Core Extraction) is **balanced truncation (Kalman/Moore 1981) operationalized for transformers**: find hidden directions that are jointly **active** (high activation covariance) and **relevant** (high downstream sensitivity), by the SVD of the interaction `H·Jᵀ` (equivalently the balanced form `SVD(LᵀΓ)` with `LLᵀ = Cov`, `ΓΓᵀ = E[JᵀJ]`), then causally validate the subspace with keep/remove/flip ablations, then identify the computation by fitting a linear operator in core coordinates. Headline results: cores are **necessary and sufficient** for tasks at 1.6–4.8% of hidden dim; they **recur across independently trained models** (geometrically near-orthogonal across seeds, yet CCA ≈ 0.99 with identical operator spectra); a single 1D steerable axis governs subject–verb agreement across six LLMs spanning two orders of magnitude of scale; IOI runs through two-site cores linked by an operator that **transfers across model families** (60% vs 77% own); and grokking time follows an inverse law `τ_grok ∝ 1/(ωp)` driven by functional redundancy under weight decay.

**Distilled for katgpt-rs (modelless, offline analysis):** the extraction RECIPE — a **balanced SVD of (activation covariance, per-sample tail-Jacobian outer products)** — is closed-form, deterministic, label-free, and its eigen kernel ALREADY SHIPS here (`basis_split::split`, Plan 625): what does not ship is the recipe itself (the `E[JᵀJ]` accumulator, the `Cov^{+1/2}` whitener, the top-eigenspace task-flow read). What ships around it: the causal validation protocol (557/778 `subspace_intervention`: aligned/random/residual), the generalized-eigen kernel (618/`basis_split`/TILR — consuming matched-pair differences, reading the INVARIANT bottom eigenspace), the supervised probe route (557 ridge-probe SVD), point-local Jacobian SVD (Plan 301), and Jacobian-image sparse projection (SPLAT). ACE's delta is the **relevance signal** (the model's own tail Jacobian, accumulated corpus-level, no labels, no pairing) and the **eigenspace** (top = task-flow, mapped back through activity — the dual of CCA's `Cov^{−1/2}`, this is `Cov^{+1/2}`). The paper also proves ACE ⊃ {PCA (isotropic sensitivity), contrastive steering (rank-1, binary, linear readout = difference of means)} — which is exactly why the delta vs our shipped probe arm is expected to be small on easy tasks and material on multi-dimensional cores with nonlinear readouts and scarce labels.

**PoC 2026-11-19 (Plan 640 Phase 1, [Bench 988](../.benchmarks/988_relevance_core_goat.md)): G0–G6 PASS** — G2 parity holds (+0.4658 vs probe@1024 +0.3335) and the G5 label-scarcity bar holds (zero-label ACE +0.4658 vs probe@8 −0.0151), so the label-free-advantage sentence STANDS on the planted fixture; disclosed: sample-Cov estimation leak along high-variance directions (∝ a_dec/√N). Promotion still needs a wired consumer.

**Verdict: GOAT — Gain (modelless track).** The extraction RECIPE — the label-free `E[JᵀJ]` relevance signal, the `Cov^{+1/2}` whitener, the top-eigenspace read — is undocumented in the workspace while the eigen KERNEL it composes ships (`basis_split`), so it files as a thin-composition primitive with a runnable proof path (the 778 planted-tiered-bank fixture extended with a planted nonlinear head, plus label-scarcity and decoy-variance arms that actually test the advantage), cheap (CPU, f64), and it composes with FOUR shipped pillars (subspace_intervention protocol, procrustes/cross_game transport, latent/subspace steering consumers, gauge_invariant adapter compose). ## 1. Paper core findings (compressed)

1. **Markov chain (3 seeds, d=64):** ACE isolates a 3D core per model — necessary (remove → chance 0.25) and sufficient (keep → baseline 0.748). Cores across seeds are **geometrically divergent** (projector overlap 0.02–0.04, principal angles 75–90°) but **statistically identical** (mean CCA ≈ 0.99) and their fitted shift operators recover the chain's exact non-trivial spectrum (0.75±0.25i, 0.50) — *functional equivalence without structural identity*, the paper's central frame (Kalman minimal realizations are unique only up to coordinate change).
2. **Modular addition (grokking):** cores crystallize AT grokking; fitted core operators' eigenvalues snap onto the unit circle (rotational/clock mechanism, recovered blind). Under continued weight decay cores **inflate** 15→50 dims — driven by *redundancy* (sufficient-dim stable; necessary-dim grows; rotational modes accumulate toward the theoretical max ⌊53/2⌋). Mechanism: min-norm under orthogonal redundant modes ⇒ `α* ∝ ψ` (Cauchy–Schwarz: every mode active). Margin ODE `ṁ = −ωm + cω‖ψ‖²` ⇒ **τ_grok = −Ω log(1 − p_crit/p) ≈ Ω·p_crit/p ∝ 1/(ωp)** — validated τ∝ω^−0.95 (R²=0.991; the WD axis itself was known — Liu et al. omnigrok [31]), ODE fit R²=0.995. Practical corollary: **interpretability window** — anneal WD to zero right after task convergence to keep the compact core.
3. **Subject–verb agreement (6 LMs, GPT-2 S/M/L, LLaMA-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B, Qwen2.5-32B):** one-dimensional core at late layers (spectral gap 40–2.8×10¹⁰), necessary (remove AUC ≤ 0.25), sufficient (keep ≥ 0.91), and **steerable**: reflecting activations through the axis inverts verb preference (AUC ≤ 0.10); an **adaptive per-token intervention** (`h̃ = h − 2s[(h−μ)ᵀq]q`, strength calibrated `s* = (m_target − m0)/g` with a verb-mass gate) inverts grammatical number throughout open-ended generation, generalizing beyond the four defining verbs. Core coordinates align **across families** (cross-model correlations 0.62–0.94, approaching the within-family ceiling 0.92–0.97). Controls: PCA-1 and the unembedding direction are sufficient-but-not-necessary; only ACE passes both.
4. **IOI (multi-step, same 6 LMs):** single-site cores are sufficient+controllable but NOT necessary (computation is distributed). Two-site cores (repeated-name s2 + final end, layers 0.25–0.50 / 0.72–0.88, within-pair difference extraction) total 1.6–4.8% of D, pass keep/flip/remove on held-out prompts; a reduced model `ẑ_E = z̄_E + A(z_S − z̄_S), m̂ = cᵀẑ_E + d` predicts the answer AUC > 0.99, and **A transfers across model families** (carried A explains 60% of the end-core swap response vs 77% own, ~0% shuffled).
5. **Meta-claims:** cores ≈ internal world models (good-regulator / internal-model principle); core orthogonality across seeds explains why naive weight merging fails, and **core alignment is a merge-compatibility diagnostic**; ACE needs white-box access (activations + gradients) — offline-cheap for any open-weights model.

## 2. Method distillation (the exact operator)

**Extract.** `H ∈ R^{N×D}` mean-centered activations; `J ∈ R^{NK×D}` stacked per-sample Jacobians of the task head `f: R^D → R^K` at each activation. SVD of the interaction `HJᵀ = UΣVᵀ`; core `= span(HᵀU_r)`, QR → orthonormal `Q`, projector `P = QQᵀ`. The efficient D×D form (what the reference implements, `_balanced_svd`): factor `Cov = HᵀH (+εI) = LLᵀ` and `S = JᵀJ = ΓΓᵀ` (eigh + sqrt + trace-relative jitter), SVD of `LᵀΓ = UΣVᵀ`, basis = `QR(L·U_r)`. The eigensystem being diagonalized is **`Cov·S`** (equivalently `S q = σ² Cov⁻¹ q`; the symmetric form `L S L = Cov^{1/2} S Cov^{1/2}` is similar to it): the eigenvalue σ² is the activity×relevance coupling, and the returned basis maps back through `Cov^{+1/2}` — weighting the read by activity. **The `Cov^{+1/2}` (not `Cov^{−1/2}`) mapping is the mathematical signature**: CCA/SVCCA whitens variance OUT (scale-free directions); ACE maps back through activity (the core lives where variance actually flows). Rank by cumulative energy of the eigenvalues or a spectral gap, refinable by causal cutoff. This closed form is a thin composition over eigen machinery that ALREADY SHIPS here — `basis_split::split` (Plan 625) is the same f64 whitened-eigensolve with a precomputed-whitener arm, rank gate, sorted descending spectra, d ≤ 16 Jacobi / otherwise `symmetric_eig` routing, d ≤ 1024 documented — so the workspace gap is the **recipe** (§3 row 1), not the kernel. The reference runs f64 end-to-end with `sample_size` 64–128 Jacobian samples (converges by 64, their Fig. A9).

**Validate.** Keep `Ph` (sufficiency), remove `h − Ph` (necessity), flip `h − 2P(h−μ)` (control), all relative to the mean μ. "Ablations test ACE; they do not construct its basis" — the ordering is closed-form; ablations pick the rank cutoff (or verify it).

**Identify.** Fit `z_{t+1} ≈ A z_t` (least squares, ridge + SVD reduction) in core coordinates; inspect eigenvalues (spectra, unit-circle modes). For IOI, the two-site cross operator of §1.4.

**Special cases (paper-proven):** isotropic `S ∝ I` ⇒ ACE = PCA; binary task + least-squares linear readout ⇒ rank-1 ACE ∝ μ₁ − μ₀ = contrastive steering (ActAdd/CAA; measured |cos| = 0.92 with the CAA axis on SVA). Both matter for us: they delimit where ACE degenerates to our shipped primitives.

**Costs:** one activation bank pass (N×D), N scalar-head Jacobians (forward-mode: K passes/sample; finite differences: D — the caller's choice; our finite-difference precedent: `subspace_phase_gate::jacobian_svd_at`, n forward evals), two D×D eigh + one D×D SVD in f64. Reference runs the Markov/modadd suite on a laptop; LLM cells on Colab.

## 4. Signal-diffs on the closest cousins (the §3.6 defenses)

1. **TILR/`basis_split` (618) — the solver IS the shipped substrate; the gap is the recipe.** `basis_split::split` already diagonalizes a whitened moment in f64 with sorted spectra, refuse-loud gates, and caller-owned zero-alloc scratch — feeding it `M = S` with a `Cov^{+1/2}` pre-whiten IS ACE's balanced form (the reviewer's arithmetic, verified: `Cov·S` and `L S L` are similar; `split` on `L S L` with `whitening = None` returns exactly ACE's spectrum and top eigvecs). What TILR does NOT ship, and what the novelty claim therefore narrows to: the label-free relevance **signal** (matched-pair differences `Δr` vs per-sample tail Jacobians `∂f/∂h` — no pairing, no labels), the **whitener sign** (`Cov^{+1/2}` activity weighting vs ZCA `Σ^{−1/2}` variance removal), and the **eigenspace read** (top = task-flow vs bottom = invariant). Different signal source, different whitener, different eigenspace — a recipe gap over an existing kernel, not an operator gap.
2. **R557/778 ridge-probe route — closest protocol cousin.** Needs a LABELED bank to fit W; aligned basis = right SVs of W (a global linear fit). ACE needs only the tail function — the model's own head — and accumulates per-sample local sensitivity `E[JᵀJ]`. On binary + linear-readout tasks they converge (paper-proven rank-1 ≡ diff-of-means; probe-W and ACE directions are near-parallel). The delta appears on: multi-dimensional cores (IOI: rank-1 arms fail, ACE rank-16+ passes), nonlinear readouts (per-sample Jacobians vary), and label scarcity. Honest posture: the probe route ships and is the incumbent; ACE files as a **second extraction arm** for the same intervention protocol, with the PoC measuring whether the label-free arm matches the supervised arm on planted geometry.
3. **R388 J-lens — the refutation, correctly scoped.** REFUTED as a `FaithfulnessProbe` **latency pre-filter** (10–70× slower; structural: n+1 evals + SVD vs 5 evals). The architectural insight (Jacobian SVD = concept basis) was explicitly preserved. ACE is an **offline census/analysis** use — different envelope entirely; the prefilter verdict does not reach it. Cited so this note never gets read as re-litigating R388.
4. **Plan 301 `jacobian_svd_at` — point-local vs corpus-level.** Ships local Jacobian SVD at ONE input (n forward evals); VMG consumes `log det(JᵀJ)` locally. ACE accumulates second moments over the corpus (`S = Σ JᵢᵀJᵢ/N`). Different grain; S-accumulation is a caller-side loop the primitive consumes (the reference's `ace_from_matrices` shape; the Rust API is `extract_core_from_matrices`).
5. **SPLAT (265) — sparse mask vs dense projector.** SPLAT selects COORDINATES (axis-aligned specialist compression, Theorem 2 sparsity bound); ACE returns a rotation-invariant SUBSPACE (dense projector P). Different geometry, different output class; both consume Jacobians.
6. **SVCCA (501/684) — the CCA dual.** CCA maps directions as `Cov^{−1/2}u` (dividing out variance); ACE maps back as `Cov^{+1/2}U` (weighting by activity). SVCCA is a similarity diagnostic between spaces; ACE is a causal intervention target in activation coordinates. The paper's own controls show CCA alignment is high even for random subspaces at low rank (their Fig. A1) — causal validation is what separates the claims, and that protocol already ships here (557).

## 8. Proof path (the pre-registered bars — full detail in Plan 640)

Fixture: the 778 planted-tiered-bank shape (synthetic, CPU, planted ground truth) EXTENDED with a planted **nonlinear** head that reads EXACTLY the planted subspace: `f = g(Q_plantedᵀ h)` with `g` a fixed elementwise nonlinearity — the head's Jacobian span is then the planted rank-r span by construction, which is the ground truth G1 scores against (with a free-form nonlinear head the relevant span would be the Jacobian's own span, not the planted one, and the bar would be scored against the wrong target). Two loud fixture refusals: constant per-sample Jacobian (the LINEAR-head degeneration — for a linear head ACE converges to probe-SVD territory per the paper's rank-1 proof, and the PoC would prove nothing beyond R557), and Jacobian span escaping the planted span.

- **G0 determinism:** BLAKE3 over the f64 (Q, energies) bit-identical ×2; f64 sequential folds (Issue 940 law — this is offline analysis, not sync-crossing, but the determinism is free).
- **G1 recovery:** ACE core at planted rank: basis similarity vs planted basis ≥ 0.90; aligned ≥ 0.9·full; residual ≤ chance + 0.05.
- **G2 label-free parity (the falsifier):** aligned-vs-random contrast at matched k — ACE arm ≥ probe arm − 0.05 (the label-free arm must not materially lose to the supervised incumbent at full labels).
- **G3 isotropic-limit identity (positive control):** on the isotropic-sensitivity variant, ACE ordering ≡ PCA ordering (paper's own special case — pins the math).
- **G4 alloc:** eval paths zero-alloc (caller scratch, the `subspace_intervention` pattern); fit-time allocs counted and disclosed.
