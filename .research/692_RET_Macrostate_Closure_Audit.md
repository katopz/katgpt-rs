## TL;DR

RET learns 128-dim **macrostates** of a frozen LLM's computation from hidden-state trajectories (BYOL/JEPA encoder-predictor, EMA teacher) and shows they satisfy three desiderata borrowed from physics: **abstraction** from microscopic detail, **approximately closed dynamics** (`I(z_{t+1}; z_t, x_t) ≈ I(z_{t+1}; z_t)`), and **practical relevance** (interpretability, early behavior prediction, causal steering). The transferable core is not the trained encoder — it is the **closure criterion as an empirical measurement**: held-out self-prediction R² of a candidate latent summary under a matched predictor-capacity sweep, compared against the same predictor given summary+microstate. That measurement is fully modelless (closed-form ridge sweep + kNN arms over a blocked-forward split), and it answers a question this stack has never measured: **is a designed latent summary (the 5 synced emotion scalars, the HLA 8-dim fingerprint, a fix-trajectory summary) actually a closed macrostate of its underlying dynamics — or is the unsynced/unread remainder load-bearing?**

**Verdict: Gain.**
- **Modelless track (primary): GOAT-eligible open primitive** — `closure_audit` (matched-capacity self-prediction gap, two-condition typed verdict), filed as katgpt-rs `.issues/1244`. NOT Super-GOAT: it is a diagnostic/measurement primitive (the Plan-342 precedent tier — 3/4 on the novelty gate; no new customer-facing behavior class), though it audits two load-bearing laws (the 5-scalar sync contract; fix-lane macrostate choice) that nothing else can measure.
- **Consumers:** riir-ai `.issues/1259` (sync-surface audit — validates or refutes the "sync only the 5 scalars, never the 64-dim vector" law with data) and riir-refine `.issues/256` (fix-trajectory early-read — rides the SHIPPED Plan-292 FutureBehaviorProbe + 301 direction-bank substrates; the closure audit validates the macrostate choice).

## 3. The extracted primitive — `closure_audit` (filed: katgpt-rs `.issues/1244`)

Matched-capacity self-prediction gap over a blocked-forward split, closed-form predictor family, two-condition typed verdict:

```
Arms (shared capacity, matched):
  A: ridge(z_t → z_{t+1}), λ swept {…} as the capacity proxy   [closed-form normal equations]
  B: ridge((z_t, x_t) → z_{t+1}), same λ grid                  [microstate-augmented]
  + kNN arms (k ∈ {1, 8, 64}) per side for local structure
Split: blocked FORWARD (train prefix windows, test suffix windows) — never an iid
       shuffle (autocorrelated trajectories leak). f64 sequential folds (Issue-940).
Gap    = R²(B) − R²(A)
Verdict: Closed     iff gap ≤ ε AND R²(A) ≥ floor     [two conditions — BOTH required]
         Lossy      iff gap > ε AND R²(A) ≥ floor     [the unsynced remainder is load-bearing]
         Undecidable iff support < floor OR R²(A) < floor  [both arms weak — never reads Closed]
```

The **two-condition verdict is the pre-registered defense of the design's known weak point**: linear+kNN arms can underfit BOTH sides; a gap ≈ 0 with weak absolute R² must read Undecidable, never a green "Closed". The paper's MLP capacity sweep is the stronger instrument if a trained predictor is ever armed (training track) — the closed-form arms are the modelless floor, disclosed in-rule.

GOAT gates: G1 three fixtures — injection arm (x_t carries next-z ⇒ gap > bar), noise arm (x_t pure noise ⇒ gap ≈ 0), identity arm (z_{t+1} = A·z_t ⇒ both arms R²=1, gap=0) — exact recoveries pinned; a planted degenerate abstraction (one summary dim a pure function of another) must COLLAPSE closure and fire Lossy/Undecidable, never pass. G2 sweep ≤ 50 ms at T=10⁴, D=64 release (offline instrument; report-only first — the `basis_purity_audit` shape). G4 zero per-tick alloc in any online consumer; bounded, disclosed offline budget. Floors pre-registered: **provenance class (v3, never OUR bars)** — App. D (RET 0.908–0.979 vs matched ceilings 0.716/0.574/0.786/0.705) and App. N (config-variant closure 0.795–0.949); OUR bars pin on the fixtures + the first real-surface census.

## 5. Training track — applicable, plan NOT filed (Research 687 precedent)
