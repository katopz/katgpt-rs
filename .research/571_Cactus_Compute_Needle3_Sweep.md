## Context in one paragraph

Cactus Compute is an on-device inference lab whose entire 2026 output (Needle 1 → 2 → 3) is a working demonstration of nearly the same bet our stack makes: **tiny attention-only models, weight-side quantization (2-bit, never-unpacked), hashed n-gram memory outside the matmul path, grammar-constrained decode, and narrow specialists that beat generalists 10× their size on the narrow task**. Needle 3 = 121M params, 70.8M of them (58%) in engram tables, one weight set where every depth 2–20 is a deployable model, 8–29 MB CQ2 binaries, 400–4k tok/s on a Pi 5. Their engineering blog reads like external validation of Plan 299 (engram), KVarN, lodestar, TernaryDraftModel, and npc_brain — and their papers contain four directly gateable primitives we don't ship yet.

## D. The Needle 3 product page (1)

**[Needle 3 — Automation Foundation Model For Tiny Devices](https://cactuscompute.com/needle)** (2026-09-18). The full-system spec the blogs split apart: 20×768, 121M params, **70.8M in engram tables (58%)**; SentencePiece BPE 8,192; **mHC 4-lane residual** — read `u = Σₙ σ(a φₙᵀ x̂ + b) Xₙ`, write `X′ = P X + 2σ(h) ⊗ y` with **P = Sinkhorn(A)** (doubly-stochastic lane write) + sandwich ZCRMSNorm; engram fusion `x ← x + σ(⟨x̂,k̂⟩/√d)·v` at sites 3/7/11/15/19, 18,432 slots, by gather; GQA 12q:2kv (48 qk / 64 v — asymmetric head dims) + 3-tap causal conv on q + RoPE + QK-norm; window 1024, **global at 4/9/14/19**, `o = σ(g)⊙softmax(...)`; Monarch Hadamard FFN; byte-level grammar; ladder 2..20 (0,19 kept); **MTP t+2 aux head (training only)**; 360B-token proprietary structured dataset. Benchmarks: Mobile Actions 961 / DroidCall 200 / BFCL v4 3,641 (AST match, no-call-on-irrelevant) / DSTC8 1,813 / SNIPS gold+7-way 700 each; **Figure 2 caveat: Needle scored through the shipped CQ2 binary, baselines at f16 under vLLM** (carries both ways — quote with the claim).

**Verdict: Gain** — a product spec, not a paper: every component has published prior art, but the **composition is a validated, priced bill of materials for our tiny-device lane** assembled from parts we ship in pieces:
- **Engram fusion ≈ our Plan 299/360 substrate, near-identical gate math** (`sigmoid_fuse_into`, τ=√d already matches, trigram hashing) — and their gate is relevance-only, i.e. **exactly the gap Issue 656 fixed on our side**; our fix is strictly better. New datum: engram tables as 58% of params (memory floor is tables, not layers — the devices post's Pi numbers confirm: "even the 2-layer model keeps most of its memory").
- **mHC Sinkhorn lane write is a new usage of shipped `sinkhorn_plan`** (3 modules ship it): balanced per-lane contribution = the kind of bridge function our sync rules want where multi-lane latent state approaches a sync boundary. riir-train xHC (Issue 383, arXiv:2607.14530) is the training-side cousin.
- 3-tap conv / GQA+RoPE+QK-norm / MTP / confidence calibration: **ship** (ShortConv1D k=3-vs-4 config delta; asentmax Qwen3 path; MTP aux-as-deep-supervision is a riir-train note).
- **New**: windowed+global σ-gated attention kernel, Monarch FFN (→569/839), depth ladder (→ladders row), byte-grammar decode, triggers-restriction.

## E. Cross-cutting findings (what the sweep says about our stack)

1. **External validation, unusually direct.** Independent lab, same year, shipped product: attention-only tiny models + hashed n-gram memory + never-unpacked 2-bit weights + grammar-constrained decode + narrow-specialist-beats-generalist + confidence-gated refusal. Our modelless bets (Plan 299 engram, ternary/Plasma, lodestar, npc_brain, refuse-never-wrong) are now corroborated by someone else's benchmark table — including the SAME failure modes we fixed (their relevance-only engram gate = our Issue 656; their uncosted router storage = our Bench 688 lesson; their "confidence is not a policy" = our evidence-tier doctrine).
2. **Four directly gateable primitives we lack**: CQ-W2A8 never-unpack GEMV (568), activation-sparse MLP decode (15), HiDRA-v2 LM head (16), functional embeddings (13). All GOAT-tier, all with concrete gates.
3. **Two training recipes with measured wins**: the depth ladder (80/20+KL, +free-regularizer effect 1.958 vs 2.037) and QAT-matched merge-at-export; both fit riir-train's existing pipeline shapes (Path 0.5, not redirects).
4. **Two boundary rules to adopt cheaply**: calibration-staleness on snapshot swap (freeze envelope invalidates attached calibrators — we serve stale plausible scores today in principle) and refusal-vs-truncation discrimination in `fix_verify` (audit first).
5. **Vendor-claim hygiene**: every Needle accuracy number is self-reported through their own engine; Figure 2 compares CQ2-shipped vs f16-baselines; "every depth is trained", the DeepSeek-V4-Flash pass, and the 4L ladder numbers must not enter a plan without our own fixtures (the x86_64-matrix lesson: a benchmark IS its fixture).
