## Why (the two confirmed gaps)

1. **Zero long-context/recall fixtures in the loop family.** Verified 2026-11-23: every loop bench/test (`gain_cost_halt_bench` — explicitly synthetic, `bench_946_infiloop`, `goat_108_lt2_looped`, `issue_035_any_time_lt2_dispatch`, `issue_407_t5`, `issue_698_t1/t4`, `issue_717_t1_t2`) is single-token, random-weights, norm/KL-shaped. The paper's YOCO-U inversion (97% of aggregate-BPB gain, 30% of long-range gain forfeited) means any future looped checkpoint graded only on our current fixtures inherits exactly that blind spot.
2. **Halting and caching are conflated.** `gain_cost_halt` answers "when do we stop" (`GainBelowCost`/`Oscillation`/`NonContraction` + QRR `LimitCycle` classification); nothing outputs "what may we cache". The paper proves the two are independent certificates (Appendix D: contractive-early-halt cores are cache-safe; fixed-depth full-depth cores are cache-*unsafe* at +0.0317 BPB under last-round KV), and its ρ_r ratio (≈0.61×/round under stochastic depth) is a per-checkpoint, provenance-free measurement of the property our `CacheStrategy::Last` (shipped `#[default]`, `katgpt-types/src/looping.rs`) silently assumes.
