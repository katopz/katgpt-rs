## Claim under test

Nestor's forgetting proposition (informal Prop 2 / formal Prop 5): after adapting on a new domain, forgetting on old labels in the FLAT predictor flows through the shared value map, and its leading coefficient scales with the principal-angle overlap between old/new value-map row spaces (cos²θ); the nested predictor's columns never move → zero forgetting on other coarse labels.

Stack translation: the SST lane (katgpt-core `sst_certified`, Issue 958; riir-ai `adapters/sst_gate.rs`, Issue 1116) already certifies delta-vs-**base** confinement (anatomy / `admit_dense`/`admit_factored` protect the protected span) and bounds same-anchor chains ADDITIVELY (`compose_tail_into`, read line-by-line at verdict round 1: it refuses anchor/shape/basis mismatches, adds the `c` payloads and the `leak_bound`s — no term compares one delta against another). What nothing measures is **delta-vs-DELTA interference inside the free tail**: two tail-confined overlays can both pass certification and still collide with each other's function — the paper's cos²θ coefficient is exactly the missing price.

**The computational fact that makes this cheap and exact (verdict round 1):** `compose_tail` only ever chains factors sharing the SAME anchor → the same orthonormal `U_⊥, V_⊥`. For `Δ = U C Vᵀ` with `VᵀV = I`: `row(Δ) = row(C)·Vᵀ` and `z ↦ zVᵀ` is an isometry (`(z₁Vᵀ)(z₂Vᵀ)ᵀ = z₁z₂ᵀ`), so the deltas' input-side principal angles equal those between `row(C₁)` and `row(C₂)` — computed on the small `f_left × f_right` payloads, never an m/n-sized QR. Dually, output-side angles equal those between the column spaces of the payloads via `U`.

**The hazard that shapes the output (verdict rounds 1–2):** a typical dense full-rank payload spans all of `R^f`, so PLAIN subspace angles between two payloads are trivially zero (cos² = 1 everywhere) whatever the deltas "mean". The PRIMARY output is therefore a per-side basis-free alignment scalar in uncentered-linear-CKA form (Gram-normalized — round-2 fix: raw-norm normalization equals `Σσ⁴/(Σσ²)²`, which reaches 1.0 only at rank 1); the cos² angle SPECTRUM is a secondary report, computed only on a rank-truncated subspace with the energy cutoff recorded beside it.

## Why not covered already (signal-diff, §3.6 evidence)

- `SstAnchor::anatomy_into` / `anatomy_factored_into` split ONE delta's energy vs the anchor — within-pair, base-protecting; no second delta appears in the formula.
- `compose_tail_into` (`sst_certified.rs:1541-1582`, read in full at verdict round 1) refuses link mismatches, adds payloads and leak bounds — NO cross-delta geometry term; the triangle-inequality leak bound prices confinement, not mutual interference.
- `certify_decoded_tail` measures one delta's decoded leak; `SstCertificate::verify_against` gates re-anchor churn. Grep: zero `principal_angle` hits in `crates/katgpt-core/src` and in riir-ai `sst_gate.rs` (verified 2026-10-29, re-verified by the verdict reviewer).

## Scope

- [ ] **T1** `sst_certified::cross_tail_interference` (feature `sst_project` — no new flag; the module's own gate): inputs two tail factors (or their payloads + shared bases); FIRST mirrors `compose_tail_into`'s refusal classes — anchor id, shape, basis digest (`ComposeMismatch` spellings) — a cross-basis comparison is meaningless without a re-factor, so it refuses loud, never re-factors silently. Computation runs on the payloads `C₁, C₂` (f_left × f_right, f64). Outputs, per side and NEVER pooled (the `anatomy` two-positive-terms discipline):
  - **input-side scalar** (the paper's value-map row-space side): uncentered linear CKA over the input-side Grams — `⟨G₁, G₂⟩_F / (‖G₁‖_F · ‖G₂‖_F)` with `Gᵢ = Cᵢᵀ Cᵢ` (f_right × f_right). Numerator = `tr(G₁G₂) = ‖C₁C₂ᵀ‖_F²` (unchanged cost vs the round-1 form); normalized by the GRAMS, not the payload norms (round-2 fix: `‖CCᵀ‖²/‖C‖⁴ = Σσ⁴/(Σσ²)²` reaches 1.0 only at rank 1 — the Gram form is 1.0 exactly when `G₁ ∝ G₂`, 0 exactly for Gram-orthogonal pairs, in [0,1] by Cauchy–Schwarz, basis-free, and still separates two different full-rank payloads).
  - **output-side scalar** (merges can also collide through `U_⊥`): the same CKA form over the output-side Grams `Hᵢ = Cᵢ Cᵢᵀ` (f_left × f_left).
  - **secondary report**: cos² spectrum from `karc::jacobi_eigen` on the symmetric PSD Gram of QR-orthonormalized truncated row/column bases, with the energy cutoff recorded in the result struct. Rank-deficient / shape-mismatch payloads refuse loud. Zero-alloc over caller scratch (the `SstScratch` posture).
- [ ] **T2** Fixture gates, built in C-COORDINATES (small f_left × f_right, no ambient matrices):
  - Givens-mixed rank-r payload pairs at θ ∈ {0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°}: spectrum matches the constructed angles (≤ 1e-12), scalars monotone in θ, EXACT zero overlap at 90° (the nesting condition), equal-payload identity (scalar 1.0);
  - **full-rank guard arm**: two DIFFERENT full-rank payloads must NOT report "identical" — the scalar separates them (this is the arm that kills the trivial-angles class; a naive angle-only spec reports 1.0 here);
  - refusal arms: mismatched anchor / basis digest / shape each refuse with the `ComposeMismatch` class.
- [ ] **T3** Predictor-vs-measured arm with PINNED UNITS and claims SPLIT BY OUTPUT (verdict round 2, point 2): the measured quantity is the normalized projection-energy fraction `‖Δ₁·P_row(Δ₂)‖_F² / ‖Δ₁‖_F²` (input side; the column-space dual for the output side) — dimensionless, ∈ [0,1], materialized from the payloads through the shared bases inside the fixture; measured values come from the paper's gradient-flow leading order via a two-step closed-form linear recursion (fixture-simulated — no shipped GD). Two separable claims:
  - **spectrum (hard bound)**: measured fraction ≤ max cos² of the secondary spectrum, with `P_row` built from the SAME truncated basis and the SAME recorded cutoff — asserted as a theorem-check. Truncation term (verdict round 3): the exact bound holds only when Δ₁'s rows lie inside its own truncated basis — true by construction for the rank-r Givens fixtures; for a payload carrying energy below the cutoff, assert `measured ≤ max cos² + ε₁` where ε₁ is Δ₁'s energy fraction below the cutoff (stored in the result struct), or project Δ₁ onto its truncated basis before measuring.
  - **scalar (no bound claim)**: held to RANKING only — correct ordering across ≥3 constructed pairs — plus the full-rank separation arm. The round-2 counterexample (C₁ rank-1 along e₁ vs C₂ = I_f: measured fraction 1, any energy-weighted scalar ≈ 1/√f) is the recorded reason the scalar carries no upper-bound guarantee.
- [ ] **T4** Determinism: f64 Gram + jacobi with sequential fold order pinned at the site (Issue-940 law); rotation-invariance spot check — an orthogonal mixing of the ambient basis (and of the payload coordinates consistent with the bases) must not move the scalars beyond 1e-10.
- [ ] **T5** Report-only API law: the instrument PRICES, never refuses (beyond the `ComposeMismatch` input guards) — riir-ai `sst_gate`'s `LegacyReported` migration posture holds until an owner-gated promotion; the consumer half (an `SstAdmissionReport` cross-tail row + optional ndb `AnchorSidecar` per-pair rows) files in riir-ai referencing this issue.

## Honest caveats

- The theorem is linear-model; on nonlinear forwards the instrument is a RANKING/predictor, never an exact prediction — gates stay fixture-exact where the theory is exact, bound-property where it is not.
- The energy-weighted scalar is an uncentered-linear-CKA ALIGNMENT PROXY with **no upper-bound guarantee on the measured projection fraction** (the rank-1-vs-identity counterexample in T3) — the bound-property claim lives on the spectrum's max cos² alone; the scalar is adjudicated on ranking and separation.
- Quantized tails: payloads computed on dequantized mirrors; the lossy-surface promotion law applies (per-family conditional retention, never bit-identity claims).
- Zero-forgetting at θ = 90° holds when adaptation support ⊆ the column's own subspace AND shared components stay frozen — real distributions approximate it; the scalar is graded, gates stay conservative.
- The instrument needs TWO persisted factors to compare — today only the anchor + the incoming delta are at hand at an install site, so the first real consumers are the freeze-delta and consolidation-delta stores (ndb) and any slot holding >1 overlay; a single-overlay site has nothing to compare against (disclosed, not a defect).
