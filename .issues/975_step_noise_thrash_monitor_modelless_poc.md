## Claim (pinned before search — Research 485 §4)

A live, modelless churn-vs-progress regime monitor (sigmoid-gated actuation-magnitude × flat-objective slope) with an evidence-per-divergence (DDR) floor schedule, wired as a third no-progress verdict row (thrash→damp+gather) in the cgsp collapse bridge and as evidence-scaled guard bounds in dao/refine — distinguished from Oudeyer-class learning-progress boredom drives (which reward LOW progress to steer exploration) by being an ANTI-thrash gate over update magnitude, and from the riir-ai bridge's existing `Trapped` row (leader-alternation flip-rate EMA, Research 374 P3) by consuming magnitude-vs-progress rather than alternation periodicity.

**Tier: GOAT-tier extraction, NOT Super-GOAT** — the adjacent class (learning-progress/boredom intrinsic motivation) is heavily published (Oudeyer 2007; Molinaro 2024); the churn×flat anti-thrash gate + DDR-as-drive are integration-novel here. This PoC decides whether the integration earns a plan.

## What the paper contributes modellessly

The PPO wrapper is incidental; the extractable content is a **step-vs-noise control law** for any anchored, evidence-driven update process: plateaus/thrash arise when the update step is large relative to evidence noise; signature = high update magnitude + high gradient/activity norm + flat objective (NOT slow learning); causal test = toggling the step mid-run induces/recovers the plateau; control knob = DDR (evidence per unit divergence from the anchor), whose optimum rises with the adaptation budget. All closed-form arithmetic over observable scalars — zero gradients.

## Primitive shapes (target module `katgpt-core/src/step_noise.rs`, feature `step_noise`)

1. `EwmaCom<const N: usize>` — EWMA center-of-mass anchor; closed-form lag law `(1−α)/α` steps (assertable).
2. `half_split_dispersion(a, b) -> f32` — 1 − cosine between the two half-window update directions; the noise proxy measured PRE-commit (proxy for gradient disagreement — labeled, not claimed as gradient norm).
3. `StepNoiseMonitor<const N>::observe(&mut self, state: &[f32; N], objective: f32) -> StepNoiseRead { churn, progress_slope, dispersion, ddr, regime }` — 4 scalars/tick, O(N), fixed arrays, zero alloc; regime via sigmoid gates `σ_churn·(1−σ_progress)` — **sigmoid, never softmax**.
4. `Regime { Learning, Thrash, Stuck, Converged, CommittedWrong }` — disjoint remedies: thrash→damp+gather, stuck→explore/replan, committed-wrong→revert, learning/converged→no-op.
5. `ddr(n_evidence: u32, divergence: f32) -> f32` (INF-guarded) + `min_ddr_at(budget_frac, lo, hi, mid, w) -> f32` — sigmoid ramp of the DDR floor with cumulative evidence budget (the paper's rising-optimum law; monotone schedule, endpoints calibrated per consumer).

## Notes

- All regime scalars stay per-process local (think-brain side); nothing latent crosses sync — only a verdict enum/gate bool would, and no listed consumer needs it to (latent/raw boundary respected).
- Proxies are labeled at the site: MCTS progress = mean-Q trend (no true objective at search granularity); belief divergence = L1-to-COM (KL proxy). Fine for gating, never claimed as the true quantity.
- Sigmoid everywhere; softmax banned. Sparse-reward regimes are OUT of scope (the source paper's own limitation) — these drives assume a measurable per-window objective signal.
