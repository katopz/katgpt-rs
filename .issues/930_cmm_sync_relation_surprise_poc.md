## Claim to prove or refute

An online second-order co-activation memory over per-entity belief dims produces a **relation-surprise** signal the Plan-277 dual-EMA value-surprise kernel cannot express: store raw `S = E_w[zz]` (upper triangle) + weighted mean `m` per ladder bank; readout computes the **mass-normalized exact covariance** `C = S/mass − (m/mass)⊗(m/mass)` and emits TWO scalars:

- `δ_level = Σ_ij|C_ij|` (+ `argmax_ij|C_ij|`) — the coupling **STATE**. High on a tightly-coupled pack, permanently. This feeds formed/released hysteresis and salience — NOT surprise.
- `δ_surprise = Σ_ij|C^fast_ij − C^slow_ij|` — the relation **CHANGE** signal across ladder banks (the second-order dual-EMA, free from the K=3 ladder). Spikes on correlation flips AND decouplings, settles after; blind to marginal movement by construction once the per-dim means settle.

⛔ Two estimator laws, both load-bearing (reviewer-verified): (i) raw products track first-order movement (z_i moving with constant coupling changes z_i·z_j for every j → ≈D× value-surprise) — everything reads C, never raw S; (ii) centering must be MASS-NORMALIZED AT READOUT, not incremental-lagged: the lagged form `(z−m)⊗(z−m)` has products in (−1,1) (z=0.9, m=0 → 0.81) and breaks the ¼ covariance bound; the mass-normalized form is exact, satisfies C_ij ∈ [−¼, ¼], and makes the A.3 divide-by-linear-weight-sum law load-bearing. **Notation law (round-3 fix): `d` is uniformly the STEP SIZE** (bank decays d ∈ {0.25, 0.0625, 0.015625} = tick/second/minute-class labels); retention is `λ = 1−d` and all updates are retention-form — `S ← λ·S + zz`, `m ← λ·m + z`, `mass ← λ·mass + 1` (recursive, no pow, no tick count) — giving `mass = (1−λⁿ)/(1−λ) → 1/d`, i.e. 4/16/64-tick effective horizons matching the labels. Mixing the two readings collapses the ladder (d-as-retention puts the "minute" bank at ~1 tick) or wrecks the normalizer — the mass formula belongs to the λ form only. A negative result is a real outcome — record it and delete the flag.
