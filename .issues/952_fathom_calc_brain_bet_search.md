## The idea (owner's, distilled)

The estimate/forecast answers should come from a **bounded calculation brain** before any heavier path:

1. **Variant bet formulas** — a "bet" is a candidate estimator composition over the comparables corpus (kNN-k variants, median-of-comps, ratio scaling, recency-weighted means, closed-form ratio regressions — each a small deterministic function).
2. **MCTS/PUCT budget over bets** — expand/evaluate bet compositions under a fixed budget `b`; each leaf bet is scored by backtest on the **selection split** of the in-state comparables (leave-one-out / cross-validation — NEVER the calibration split; the picked bet → point + spread → `ConformalIntervalCalibrator` overlay reads its residuals from a SEPARATE calibration split. Selecting the estimator on the calibration rows breaks exchangeability and makes the stated coverage optimistic — the exact failure the Report-the-Floor gate exists to catch). Deterministic seeded, zero-alloc core, budget-bounded. When comps are too few to split (n ≤ ~8): jackknife+/cross-conformal, or the plain floor serves (the threshold derives from the T4 comps-size axis, never a hand-typed edge).
3. **Finishes before the *NN way or freeze/thaw** — budgeted search is µs–ms class by construction vs training or a snapshot swap. The search IS the interim answer path; freeze/thaw + trained heads are the later lanes.
4. **Sigmoid gate → consolidation later** — every evaluated bet's score gates into the outcomes ledger / strategy routing as the FLOOR-RELATIVE SKILL, `sigmoid(β·(CRPS_floor − CRPS_bet)/CRPS_floor)` — CRPS alone is lower-is-better AND scale-dependent, so raw CRPS values are not comparable across comps sets or across states; the normalized skill is. Consolidation happens later, not at answer time. **Determinism law (Issue 940):** any bet score or pick that is committed, hashed, or replay-compared through the ledger pins the sequential fold and a fixed first-index tie rule — otherwise the ledger intake is marked local-only at the call site.
5. **Calculation before model-based** — the calculation brain's outputs (typed values, candidate bets with scores, intervals) ride escalations to model-based lanes as instruction context or as extra candidate samples the model ranks/picks.

## Honest caveats (verdict-reviewed)

- **The wasm VEHICLE is the attestation/portability form, NOT the speed claim.** The workspace's own numbers: bench 205 wasmi ~46× slower than V8 JIT (same moves, bit-parity); riir-refine wasm-validator verdict measured native 21 ns vs wasm 5.99 µs ≈ 285× ("native is fine" — the lane stays opt-in). Serving path = NATIVE compiled code; wasm32 strategy packs are the distribution/attestation form (verifiable artifacts, instinct-arsenal A2 hot-swap compatible, untrusted-pack safe). Claiming wasm for speed would be a false GOAT.
- **Latency bar:** the search budget must size to the G2 p99 bar (Plan 628 T0.6); bench 205's batching negative (compute-bound, 1.09–1.19×) says do NOT bet on batch rescues — budget the tree, not the batch.
- **The bet family is authored, not learned** (modelless-first): each bet formula is a deterministic authored function; the search composes/selects, it never trains.
- **GOAT gates:** G1 = must beat the conformal-naive floor (the bet search composes WITH the floor; losing bets demote to floor) **with the selection/calibration split intact — a coverage number produced on merged comps is not a measurement**; the search must ALSO earn its budget: **control (d) = exhaustive/uniform search at the EQUAL budget** — 4–6 bets with shallow composition is a space of tens of candidates that fits inside b=64; if exhaustive ties PUCT at equal budget, the exhaustive version SHIPS and PUCT waits for larger bet families. G2 = budget-bounded p99; G4 = zero-alloc tree (fixed-arity node pool).
