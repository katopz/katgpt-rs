- [ ] **C1 — divergence-threshold gate family.** `D_switch > τ` takeover between a cheap generator and a strong one: JSD (symmetric, exact ceiling ln 2 ⇒ normalized [0,1] sigmoid input, no per-suite recalibration), forward/reverse KL (unbounded — direction semantics: fwd = student missing modes, rev = student overconfident mass), entropy, top-K intersection. Consumer: any dual-lane generative surface (spec-decode drafter/verifier is the nearest shipped genus — signal-diff: LeviathanVerifier accepts AFTER drafting, this gates BEFORE).
- [ ] **C2 — inflection provenance substrate.** Per-position provenance rows (source, divergence, reason) as receipts; inflection-count as a cheap-lane failure predictor. Gate: AUC of inflection-count predicting failure on a held-out capture > 0.5 + CI floor. Dynamics rider (track-b residue): escalation-rate decay on recurring families after corpus folding vs no-fold control — a dynamics signal only where mutated state exists (stationary surfaces take LEVEL readouts, never slope).
- [ ] **C3 — positional supervision-signal diagnostic.** Divergence-vs-position bucket census along rollouts/traces; the paper's 3.3×/17× numbers are LM-scale claims — re-measure locally, never transfer on authority. Gate: early/late concentration ratio reported with bootstrap CIs; an inverted ratio is a finding, not a failure. Cognition reading: "conditioning influence washes out along a rollout" independently supports the stale-belief sigmoid-decay pattern in the game lane (note-level pointer only).
- [ ] **C4 — post-hoc τ-sweep / escalation-band instrument.** Log per-position divergences once; the entire τ sweep is then post-hoc on the frozen capture (stationary divergence field — the frozen two-lane serving posture is MORE favorable than the paper's training setting). Gate: monotone escalation-rate decay in τ + knee disclosure. Consumer: `seat_rerank`/ESC escalation-band tuning (decision-level analog; τ band ↔ escalation-rate band).
- [ ] **C5 — hidden-state wire transfer.** Ship the producer's final post-norm hidden state (2,560 floats on the paper's 4B) instead of full logits (151,936 ⇒ ~59×), project at the receiver with the producer's output head (BLAKE3-pinned artifact in a frozen posture — no refresh needed). Gate: G5-parity first (projected vs on-device logits within pinned fp tolerance), then bytes/token + joint throughput ≥ ~0.9×; regime note: wins when bandwidth-bound, loses when the receiver head-multiply dominates. Consumer surface: riir-infer plan-623 multi-host wire (STAGED/opt-in — re-open this item only if that lane lands a cross-host activation channel).
- [ ] **C6 — sparse lower-bounded KL (the crown jewel).** Store raw pre-temperature logits at union(topK(teacher), topK(student)) indices + the full-vocab logsumexp; kept entries exact (`log q(v) = logits[v]/T − lse`), dropped tail one bucket; KL over the partition is a LOWER BOUND on true KL by the log-sum inequality (error sign KNOWN), tightening in K; `kept_mass` = coverage floor. Union-of-both-top-Ks is load-bearing (single-side truncation cuts exactly the terms the other direction is made of — measurable one-sided underestimate). Numerics riders (property tests, permanent): finite sentinel `-1e9` not `-inf` (`0·inf=NaN` in masked reductions), tail clamp 1e-12 before `log`, capture raw logits never rescaled log-probs. Gate: bound-tightness curve (gap shrinks monotonically in K), kept_mass floors, decision-agreement rate vs full-vocab at equal threshold decisions. Consumer: any O(V)-prohibitive divergence gate; verify-cache posture C7 is its validation doctrine.
- [ ] **C7 — verify-cache posture + probability-weighted cache error.** Three postures off/verify/on; verify runs cache + fresh forward and logs reproduction error weighted by the producer's probability (errors on never-emitted tokens discount to zero). Gate: probability-weighted error ≤ bar before a cache posture promotes to `on`; alignment (large sporadic) vs truncation (small systematic) errors separated. Generalizes to index/retrieval/belief caches.
