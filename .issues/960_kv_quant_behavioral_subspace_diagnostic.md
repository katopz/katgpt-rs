# Issue 960 — KV-quant behavioral-subspace diagnostic + sensitivity-guided bit allocation (modelless PoC)

**Status:** OPEN — PoC proposed from Research 633 (arXiv:2606.09864, the KV-quant alignment-collapse law); serving-time + training-free. Surfaces (verified in-tree 2026-10-24): the six bit-width quantizer backends — `crates/katgpt-quant/src/{turboquant,planar_quant,iso_quant,hybrid_oct_pq,octopus}/kv_cache.rs` (the landed `katgpt-quant` crate, Proposal 001) + `crates/katgpt-kv/src/kvarn/kv_cache.rs` (KVarN) — plus `crates/katgpt-spectral/src/spectral_kv_cache.rs` (SpectralQuant, per-dim variable-bit packing) and `crates/katgpt-kv/src/shard_kv/kv_cache.rs` (ShardKV, asymmetric K/V compression); trait `crates/katgpt-types/src/kv_cache.rs`. Out of scope: `osc_kv` (IMEX oscillatory states — lossy but not a bit-width quantizer) and `fitted_value_table` (value table, Bench 895). The `kv_sensitivity_alloc` feature homes in `crates/katgpt-spectral` (beside the water-fill allocator it extends — T2); a weight input inside a crate `katgpt-kv` already deps changes no boundary edge — no per-crate BOUNDARY.md exists, the repo-root `BOUNDARY.md` is the contract.

## Problem

The KV-quant family is GOAT'd on PPL + task suites (KVarN Bench 053 ran AIME24/MATH500/HumanEval/IFEval), but has **no diagnostic** for the failure mode the lossy gate's external validation #4 shows dominates at low bits: behavioral subspaces 10²–10³× more quantization-fragile than the PPL-averaged space. Today, if a backend fails a family-conditional retention read, the only path forward is brute-force bit-width sweeps — no answer to *which channels/layers carry the damage* or *which mitigation direction* (finer granularity vs precision floor vs allocation spread). The paper's PCR diagnostic predicts the correct mitigation from ~20 calibration prompts, training-free.

## Proposed PoC (all serving-time, zero gradient descent)

- [ ] **T0 — sensitivity probe.** Per-channel quantization-vs-reference delta over `QuantizedKVCache`: quantize each KV channel (per layer, per K/V side) against its f16 reference on ~20 calibration prompts; rank channels by **behavioral damage** (attention-pattern delta / downstream pick flip), never MSE alone (MSE is the wrong metric — KVarN's own finding, now with a behavioral weighting). Deterministic, zero-alloc probe, cached per (model, backend, bits).
- [ ] **T1 — failure-mode classification.** Map the sensitivity profile to the paper's three modes, translated to non-safety vocabulary: *outlier-crushes-behavior* (behavior in non-outlier channels, collateral of outlier-driven scales) / *behavior-in-outliers* (finer granularity cannot rescue) / *multi-layer dilution* (spread across layers, per-layer fixes fail). Output: the predicted mitigation direction, with the paper's 20-prompt budget.
- [ ] **T2 — behavioral-damage-weighted bit allocation.** Spend the bit budget on the top-k fragile channels/layers. **Substrate-first: the allocator already ships — `katgpt-spectral` exports `BitAllocator` + `waterfill_bits` (spectral_kv_cache.rs:11; manifest: "Lloyd-Max / water-fill bit allocation … Shared by katgpt-kv (ShardKV, KVarN)") — so the feature is a BEHAVIORAL-DAMAGE WEIGHT INPUT to the existing water-fill, homed in `katgpt-spectral` (the shared substrate katgpt-kv already deps; `katgpt-quant` has no edge to katgpt-kv/spectral and gains none).** The delta over the shipped allocator is the weighting: SpectralQuant weights by STRUCTURAL rank (top-`d_eff` semantics-first); T2 weights by behavioral damage from T0's probe. If the two weightings correlate on our models, that correlation is itself a T3 finding. Feature-gated (`kv_sensitivity_alloc`, opt-in, home `katgpt-spectral`). **GOAT controls (both arms, equal average bits): (1) uniform allocation; (2) the shipped structural-rank weighting.** A win vs f16-at-equal-bytes alone is not the claim.
- [ ] **T3 — behavioral-retention eval.** Axes: PPL (expected flat — that is the point), decision-suite pick-flip rate (the reflex harness suites at the pinned engine baseline), IFEval-style instruction-following. Record whether *PPL-blind / behavior-visible* reproduces on our stack and at which bit-widths the phase transitions sit per model.
- [-] **Refusal/safety axis** — deferred: needs an instruct/safety-tuned model (gemma-2-2b-it RETIRED 2026-10-07; none pinned locally). The default PoC runs on the decision/base models we hold; the refusal axis re-opens when a model pin lands.

## Acceptance (GOAT)

- [ ] **G1 correctness (instrument, not gain):** probe deterministic (fixed prompt set + fixed seeds); classification stable across prompt resamples within the disclosed band. A T3 negative result must be readable as a finding about the models, never as the instrument being broken.
- **G2 perf/memory (the gain claim, both controls):** the behavioral-damage weighting recovers retention at lower average bits than BOTH (1) uniform allocation and (2) the shipped structural-rank weighting — or matches both at equal bits with a strictly better retention profile; `--release` mandatory.
- **G3 no regression:** lossless path bit-identical; feature-off byte-identical boots.
- **G4 alloc:** allocation LOOKUP on the decode path is zero-alloc in the steady state. The ~20-prompt probe itself is a CALIBRATION step (one-shot, like `sst_certified`'s f64 Gram fit) — it is not held to the steady-state standard.
- **Report-the-floor:** retention numbers quote BOTH floors beside them — uniform-at-equal-bits AND structural-rank-at-equal-bits — a comparison against f16 alone hides the ordering claim this issue makes.

## Honesty notes

- Our models are base/decision models: the paper's *safety-refusal* evidence is theirs; ours is suite-pick retention + instruction-following. The mechanism claim (subspace fragility) is expected to transfer; if T3 shows NO PPL-blind/behavior-visible separation on our models, record the negative honestly — that is a real result about where the law binds (behavioral-subspace concentration may be a safety-tuning artifact, which the paper's own model-family spread can partially already answer).
- Phase-transition localization is per-model by the paper's own finding — no universal bit-width recommendation ships from this issue; the deliverable is the diagnostic + the allocation *procedure*.
