## Problem

The KV-quant family is GOAT'd on PPL + task suites (KVarN Bench 053 ran AIME24/MATH500/HumanEval/IFEval), but has **no diagnostic** for the failure mode the lossy gate's external validation #4 shows dominates at low bits: behavioral subspaces 10²–10³× more quantization-fragile than the PPL-averaged space. Today, if a backend fails a family-conditional retention read, the only path forward is brute-force bit-width sweeps — no answer to *which channels/layers carry the damage* or *which mitigation direction* (finer granularity vs precision floor vs allocation spread). The paper's PCR diagnostic predicts the correct mitigation from ~20 calibration prompts, training-free.
