# Issue 982 — Cross-path delta census + long-horizon byte-exactness soak (the premise gate from arXiv:2611.05345)

**Status:** OPEN — premise-gate experiment, T0 runnable now in minutes (numerics measurement on the existing verify path, NOT acceptance work — the 746/747 acceptance-work closure is untouched); the acceptance-gate arm of this issue (T7/T8) is additionally owner-gated behind the riir-ai Plan 614 kernel bar AND closes as unmotivated if T0 reads structural zero.

**Source:** [Research 652](../.research/652_Adversarial_Speculative_Reasoning_Precision_Allocator.md) — arXiv:2611.05345 "Adversarial Speculative Reasoning Improves Long Horizon Agents" (Luo et al., 3 Nov 2026, CC BY 4.0). The paper measures: verification blocks are lossless in distribution but noise grows ~4%/slot (slot 0 == decode; >2-nat tails ~2× by block end), and verify-written context shows 10–20% excess >4-nat deviations after ~30 turns. Closed form: `Pr[reject] = TV(p,q)` — the draft controls where blocks end.

## Why this issue exists (the crux)

riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1 records *"Lossless by construction (verify is exact) — G1 byte-identity holds."* The paper forces the unpacking: lossless-in-**distribution** is the rejection-sampling theorem (noise-independent); **byte-identity** vs serial is a per-run assertion — the verify pass computes slot-s logits within a block launch (slot s attends to same-launch KV entries), the serial pass from a single-token launch over cache-read entries, and these differ bitwise **unless the kernels are batch-invariant** (reduction order fixed regardless of launch shape — the Thinking-Machines property). For our lane that property is **UNVERIFIED**: Bench 973's byte-exactness through 2048 tokens (incl. the G-correction arm's 95 forced rollbacks) is consistent with flip-freeness at that horizon but does not establish batch-invariance of the logits themselves. **T0 below settles existence in minutes**; every downstream arm is conditional on its answer.

riir-ai Bench 973 proved byte-exactness at a **2048-token horizon** (incl. forced j=1 rejection every cycle, 95 real rollbacks + journal replay, byte-identical — also the exactness half of any future forced-rejection gate). The paper's divergence regime is 30+ turns / up to 128k further tokens: **the long-horizon half is unmeasured**. One mechanism correction (verdict round 1): the naive "Q4_K_M weights are lossier so our noise is bigger" is WRONG — weight quantization is deterministic and identical on both paths, contributing no slot-path delta. The cross-path delta comes from launch-shape differences only (KV write path, reduction order, batch-W-vs-1 matmul tiling); its magnitude here is UNMEASURED and no above/below prediction is claimed.

**Decisive question, cheap-first order — T0 first:** does the verify launch differ bitwise from the serial launch at the same logical positions? Zero everywhere → our kernels are batch-invariant for these shapes → byte-exactness is STRUCTURAL, the premise closes favorably (gate-validity confirmed structurally; recorded in Plan 614 + riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1; T7/T8's motivation gone — honest closure). Nonzero → the noise channel exists; T1 measures the delta profile by slot; if material, T4 soaks the walk: does any keyed argmax flip occur at the registered bar? No flips → validity MEASURED AT THAT BAR, adversarial training arm stays dead (riir-train Issue 672 T0). Flips → the byte-exact gate needs redefinition (per-step losslessness vs whole-trajectory identity are different claims — Plan 623 T5.1's sentence currently conflates them), and both the paper's mechanism and the modelless placement gate go live on our stack. (DCOCO/R636 pattern: the premise gate orders FIRST.)

## Tasks

- [ ] **T0 — structural existence check (minutes, decides the premise)**: one bitwise compare of verify-launch logits vs serial-launch logits at the same logical positions (the next W draft-block tokens placed at slots 0..W−1 after an identically-written serial prefix; compare each slot's distribution against the serial single-token forward for that position), a few prompts × seeds × positions, both block widths k ∈ {6,8}. **Exact-zero everywhere** → kernels are batch-invariant for these shapes → premise CLOSED (structural byte-exactness; record the verdict in riir-ai Plan 614 + riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1, splitting the "lossless by construction" sentence into its two halves explicitly; T1/T4 become confirmatory-only and T7/T8 close as unmotivated — an honest favorable verdict). **Nonzero anywhere** → the noise channel exists; proceed to T1.
- [ ] **T1 — cross-path delta census (only if T0 is nonzero)**: `Δ_path(q,s) = |logp_verify-launch(s) − logp_serial-launch(s)|` per slot over identically-written prefixes; emit per-slot mean AND per-slot exceedance tables P[Δ>τ] for τ ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 4} nats (tails, not just slopes — the paper's tails grow ~2× while the mean grows 34%) + bootstrap CIs over (prefix × position). Fixture: ≥2 prompt classes (doc-prose + code), 6k–24k-token contexts, matching the paper's census posture. On a deterministic stack Δ_path is a FIXED path-dependence profile per (prefix, position, seed), not a random variable — the statistics live in the variation across positions, which is what the bootstrap measures.
- [ ] **T2 — determinism + identity + canary arms (instrument validation)**: (a) the paper's repeat-forward arm (R=4 identical forwards) must read ZERO on our stack — Bench 973 G-determinism says it will; a nonzero here is itself a FINDING (nondeterministic kernels — a lane bug, not a census result); (b) slot-0-vs-serial identity check at matched prefix; (c) shuffled-slot-labels arm reads flat (instrument canary).
- [ ] **T3 — precision-cliff THEORY-TRANSFER probe (report-only, ASSUMPTION-MARKED)**: whether Issue 959's per-accumulator cliff (`precision_cliff` = p·ln2 nats, `../crates/katgpt-core/src/softmax_fix.rs`) is the right theory for a verify-vs-decode accumulation-order delta is UNESTABLISHED — this task probes the transfer, never gates on it. Probe: the slot-slope over s≥1 and/or the per-same-launch-write rounding size vs the KV-format rounding scale. N(0) is NOT compared against the cliff (on a batch-invariant stack it is structurally zero — comparing a structural zero against a format floor is meaningless). Canary: a deliberately mismatched format tag must red.
- [ ] **T4 — long-horizon soak (bar: ≥32k tokens minimum + one 128k stretch arm, ≥4 seeds)**: keyed-spec walk vs serial keyed decode on the same seed, byte-compare + AUDIT MODE (count and locate divergences instead of failing the run), doc-prose + code corpora. Map the bar onto the paper's regime explicitly: its ~30-turn horizon is a READ-BACK drift regime over multi-turn agent trajectories — the single-stream walk approximates it by token count; where feasible, run a teacher-forced read-back analog (context written via verify vs via decode, same tokens, then score the continuation — the paper's §3.2 second method). **Include the re-encode isolation ablation in this task**: re-materialize the same committed tokens through fresh slot-0 passes — if verify-written ≈ re-encoded, the excess is content, not write-path numerics (the kill test for the whole precision story on our stack).
- [ ] **T5 — flip census adjudication (claim matched to the bar)**: flips concentrate on high-`Δ_path`/low-keyed-margin positions (separation ≥5× vs unflagged) or the fragility model is FALSIFIED (either verdict is a win — the question is decided, not begged). Zero flips at the registered bar → the byte-exact gate's long-horizon validity is MEASURED **AT THAT BAR** (never claimed beyond it); record in riir-ai Plan 614 + riir-infer Plan 623 T5.1 (per-step vs trajectory claim split made explicit).
- [ ] **T6 — TV(p,q) emission (cheap, independent)**: emit per-position `TV(p,q) = 1 − Σ min(p,q)` (top-k support + closed tail bound) alongside accept/reject in the keyed walk + a per-slot `E[TV]` EMA in `correlation_budget.rs` (Plan 200). GOAT: empirical reject rate over ≥10⁴ seeds within binomial CI of TV per position; falsification arm: perturb q by ε, assert TV moves by the predicted ε-class amount.
- [ ] **T7 — noise-scaled forced-rejection/deferral gate (DESIGN ONLY this issue; implementation is owner-gated behind the riir-ai Plan-614 kernel bar AND closes as unmotivated if T0 reads structural zero)**: on trigger (lexicon frame-hit ∨ target-verify entropy above trailing-window Q90 ∨ margin below Q10 — self-calibrating quantiles, no magic constants; or the derived flag `Δ_path(s, τ=2) ≥ α·m` — ⚠ NOT a live measurement: computing Δ_path at runtime would need a serial forward alongside every verify; the flag reads a per-slot LOOKUP TABLE fitted offline from the T1 census (the §4 row-1 "fitted table → runtime constant" shape)), DEFER: end the block so the decision token is written at slot 0 of the next forward. Same target positions and forward count; only boundaries move. Ablation pair pre-registered: in-place resample (the paper's semantics) vs deferral (ours — strictly better on the paper's own precision axis). Pre-registered bars: OFF / DIRECT / INVERTED / RANDOM-at-matched-rate arms (the placement-beats-rate template from Research 652 §4 row 9); DIRECT ≥ OFF + 1.0 pt at ≤2% throughput cost; DIRECT > RANDOM paired LB95 > 0; INVERTED ≤ OFF + 0.5; deferral ≥ in-place on read-back noise. KILL: DIRECT ≈ RANDOM → decision-point mechanism dead on this stack, arm retired; INVERTED > OFF → signal is noise.
- [ ] **T8 — frame-lexicon detector (modelless, rides T7's arm structure)**: token-id sets for principle/evidence vs likely/risk/simpler markers (the paper's taxonomy as code); validated against a judge-labelled trajectory sample with the paper's 70%/36% split as the label-quality check; recall on confidently-wrong (low-entropy) frames is the FIRST number produced — if lexicon ∪ entropy recall is poor, the expected gain (recall × flip-rate × ~34 pts) is bounded below useful and the arm closes honestly.
