## Background

The paper robustifies any black-box sequential predictor into a low-regret one via a deterministic stopping-time hard switch to a Polya-urn (≡ Hedge) reference, at TV budget |A|·T^(−α), and proves same-window robustification impossible (de Bruijn families) while Δ extra context buys O(1/√Δ) regret. Workspace signal-diffs (Research 647 §3): reflex `CorpusDistanceGate`/`SigmoidGateCalibrator` and rethink's ESC rate guard consume geometry and escalation-rate signals — none consumes *realized adversarial regret vs a no-regret reference*; OPUS/EqR/BanditPruner track *stochastic* pseudo-regret (Plan 030 documents the adversarial exclusion); Raven ships the gate→fallback shape without a theorem; `causal_id`'s "hedge" is a graph term (vocabulary collision). Prior-art verdict: zero kills (the published claims — the theorems — are the paper's own and novel as published); our contribution here is the signal class (realized adversarial regret vs a no-regret reference — no shipped gate consumes it) plus the serving-lane wiring, which is engineering, not a novelty claim — the published escalation neighborhood (2305.05176, 2610.22695, 2610.07816, 2607.05438) is practice-only and must be cited in any consumer doc.

## Scope

Opt-in primitives in `katgpt-core` (proposed feature `regret_guard`; re-run the substrate-first grep at implementation time — `hedge` greps hit `causal_id` bow-arc, `regret` hits bandit crates; candidate pre-existing spellings: `adversarial_stream`, `reference_leg`, `fallback_band`), re-exported at the root. Zero-alloc scratch per `gaussianity_probe`.

- [ ] **T1. Core gate.** `RegretGapGate`: incremental Polya-urn counts + exp-weights reference leg (λ_t = √T/(|Θ|+t−1)), running utility sums for the frozen leg (QBR = temperature-η action distribution — mandatory per Lem 2.2: a deterministic best response is Ω(1)-exploitable), per-round gap = avg-regret(frozen) − avg-regret(urn), sticky OR-latch when `gap ≥ (log|A|)/√T + √(8(1+α)(log T)/s)` — index pin, Alg 1 verbatim: the Azuma root term runs over the switch-prefix length **s**, the log|A| term over **T** (Lem 6.1's per-round quantal price; Thm 7.1 restates the same band) — switching the prediction source to the urn from the next round on. O(1)/round, no allocation steady-state. ⛔ The exp family is LOAD-BEARING on BOTH legs: the reference leg IS Hedge by Lem 2.3, and the frozen leg's QBR action distribution is exp-family by definition (an attention softmax computes it in Thm 5.1) — "sigmoidifying" either leg's action distribution silently voids the regret bounds. Documented softmax exception class: the two legs' QBR action distributions + Hedge weights only; sigmoid stays on every gate/threshold/projection/mixing. Loud refusal when U is not supplied (the U-free escape is T2's binary detector only).
- [ ] **T2. V-drift detector (binary, U-free).** V-grid ℓ_v(p)=−|p−v| over an ε-grid; per-prefix max-V-regret with the concentration threshold √(log(N_ε t²/δ)/t); per-window drift scalar + fired bit. Scope pin: binary only — the |Θ|≥4 decomposition fails (Kleinberg et al.), refuse loud on wider alphabets rather than silently computing the cover.
- [ ] **T3. Falsification arms** (the pre-registered proof path; every arm is a failing-loud test):
  - deterministic-BR probe (Lem 2.2): unswitched frozen predictor under θ_t = 1 − a_t reads Ω(1) regret; gate-armed reads sublinear — pins that the quantal leg is mandatory;
  - 8-distribution battery (Ber(1/3), Ber(2/3), both regime-switch orders, sine drifts ϕ ∈ {T/2, T/5, T/10, T/20}): in-distribution fidelity preserved (d_NT small, negative in-distribution regret class) AND adversarial sequences sublinear — the no-harm arm;
  - TV-budget arm: switching-event probability ≤ |A|·T^(−α) posture at seeded adversarial draws; fidelity axis is **d_NT, never full-sequence TV** (the 0.42 same-seed law, Research 647 §1);
  - de Bruijn certificate arm: successor/complement order-L family; assert every L-window predictor on the complement stream reads ≥ 1/24 regret or the successor reads ≥ 1/24 TV-farness — **the un-escalated arm's red IS the theorem firing**;
  - Δ-context arm: suffix-averaged robustifier (Alg 2, action-space averaging, \|A\|=2 pullback) regret decreasing ~ 1/√Δ across Δ ∈ {8, 32, 128}.

GOAT gate.** G1 = the T3 arms (a passing gate means: fires where unswitched/incumbent-silent, stays silent in-distribution); G2 ≤ µs-class per round O(1) incremental at --release; G3 default-feature count unchanged; G4 zero-alloc steady state. **Promotion stays opt-in pending a consumer measurement** (Issue 978 precedent): the gate is real when a lane shows the firing case with an incumbent-silent ground truth.

## Disclosures

- The gate needs realized outcomes + a known utility table; single-shot decision serving without sequential feedback has no regret signal — wiring onto a non-sequential consumer is a category error, don't.
- The certificate is pre-flight (existence over a family at a context width), the gate is runtime (being-beaten-now) — never conflate; a green certificate does not clear the runtime gate and vice versa.
- Stochastic-regret bandit lanes (Plan 030 scope) are unchanged by this issue.
