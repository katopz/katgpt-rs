## Premise

The shipped modality-additive belief kernel `evolve_belief_additive` (katgpt-sense `reconstruction.rs`, `modality_additive` default-on) is exactly the contracting discrete cell of arXiv:2611.09217: its continuous residual field has Jacobian **−(1−α_m)·I** (bounded sigmoid drive, α_m = `belief_retention[m]`) — uniformly monotone, exact — and its discrete map contracts with factor ρ = α_m, κ_m = −ln α_m (bridge between spaces: δκ ↔ 1−α_m; see Research 667 §4.1). The kernel therefore carries fading-memory, invariant-ball, and tunability guarantees **by construction** — but no surface asserts, measures, or derives from them. Three constants are hand-pinned and mutually uncoupled: `belief_retention: [0.9; 6]` (κ ≈ 0.105/tick, half-life ≈ 6.6 ticks at 20 Hz), `ObservationGate` λ = 0.05 (riir-engine — the consumer seam, not edited here), `additive_drive_scale` = 4.0. The divisive kernel `leaky_step` is NOT a contraction (state-independent delta, clamp-boundary fading) — recorded as a distinction in 667 §4.1, never a defect; do not "fix" it.
