## Why these two

1. **Snap-step τ.** The paper binarizes every recurrent state through `τ(z) = ReLU(z) − ReLU(z−1)` — flat 0 for z ≤ 0, flat 1 for z ≥ 1, a linear ramp between — and engineers every argument to land at ≥ 1.5 or ≤ −0.5, so any per-step numeric error below 0.5 leaves the output bit-unchanged (the mechanism behind ~2 million drift-free loops). **Signal-diff vs the nearest cousins (narrowed per verdict round 1 — the reviewer's grep found shipped ε-dead-bands; coordinator-verified this session):** ε-dead-bands DO ship — `DEFAULT_GAP_EPS` (`katgpt-core/src/mux/height_gate.rs` L55, flat tolerance, `dead_band_holds` test L449), `DEFAULT_QUANT_EPSILON` (`../riir-ai/crates/riir-engine/src/policy_cache/quantize.rs` L30 — ±ε ternary sign dead-band, `dead_band_produces_all_zero_digits` + `custom_epsilon_widens_dead_band` tests), and `katgpt-transformer/src/dense_mesh/adaptive_width.rs` (reviewer-found). What is genuinely NEW is narrower, two parts: (a) **parking values deliberately ≥ 0.5 outside a [0,1] ramp** so that step-by-step error cannot build up over long loops (the existing dead-bands suppress noise near a boundary; τ's contract parks the STATE ITSELF outside the ramp and guarantees invariance over an unbounded loop count), and (b) a **format-aware audit of that margin** (ULP × accumulation terms — none of the existing dead-bands checks its ε against the float format).
2. **Exact-head margin audit.** The paper's exact-match attention heads achieve hard selection by score gap alone (`s = 100` ⇒ the intended key outscores every competitor by 100; softmax leak `e^−100`). The CHECKING law ships — `margin_equilibrium(eps, FloatFormat)` + `precision_cliff` (`softmax_fix.rs`, Issue 959; verified at L43/L75/L91/L116 this session) — but nothing composes them into a construction-time verdict for a written head: given (score gap, competitor count, accumulation terms, format), is the pick numerically stable at that format? The audit fn is the missing composition; the paper is its worked instance (a 100-nat gap vs the Fp8E4M3 cliff 4·ln₂ ≈ 2.77 nats ⇒ ~36× headroom before any accumulation bound).
