## Scaling shape (the load-bearing facts)

- Every matvec-class arm is **E-linear**: circulant rungs scale ~×16 per node-doubling of edges; planted rungs (dense cliques) pay ~27× the sparse cost per node. The "≤1k nodes" framing in the plan bar was node-framed; the cost is EDGE-framed — a fixture-shape dependence the plan bar did not name.
- The walk pick is **k² × similarity**: 276 calls at k=23, 1 128 at k=47 — per-step-unusable at planted degree (ms→s per pick), the consumer-profile input for the riir-ai Plan 646 family (which wires the BATCH selector, not the walk pick).
- Spectral queries are O(k=n) gathers with a build-time O(n³) Jacobi — µs-class per query at every cap-legal size; the build cost (362–602 ms at n=256) rides the per-batch class.
